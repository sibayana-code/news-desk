Gospel of the day

From the Gospel according to Luke 8:19-21

The mother of Jesus and his brothers came to him but were unable to join him because of the crowd. He was told, “Your mother and your brothers are standing outside and they wish to see you.” He said to them in reply, “My mother and my brothers are those who hear the word of God and act on it.”

The words of the Popes

Jesus continued speaking with the people, and He loved the people and loved the crowd, so much so that He said, “These who follow me, that vast crowd, are my mother and my brothers; they are these”. And He explained: [They are] those who listen to the Word of God and put it into practice. These are the two conditions for following Jesus: listening to the Word of God and putting it into practice. This is Christian life, nothing more. Simple, simple. (…) Every time we do this – we open the Gospel and read a passage, and ask ourselves: “Is God speaking to me with this, is He saying something to me? And if He is saying something, what is He saying?” – this is listening to the Word of God, listening to it with our ears and listening to it with our hearts. Opening our hearts to the Word of God. Jesus’ enemies listened to His words, but they stayed close to Him to try and find a mistake, to trip Him up, and to undermine His authority. Yet they never asked themselves: “What is God saying to me in this Word?” And God does not speak merely to everyone: yes, He speaks to everyone, but He speaks to each one of us. The Gospel was written for each and every one of us. (…) The Lord sows His Word, always; He merely asks for an open heart to hear it and a willingness to put it into practice. Let us therefore make today’s prayer that of the Psalm: “Lead me in the path of thy commandments”, that is, the path of Your Word, so that, with your guidance, I may learn to put it into practice. (Pope Francis, Homily of 23 September 2014)

Word

heed /hiːd/ — careful attention, notice, or consideration; to pay attention to and act on what is heard or warned.

From Old English hēdan “to take care, observe, attend to,” related to German hüten “to guard.”

heed — Jesus names as family those who hear the word of God and act on it. Francis: Christian life is listen, then put into practice. Way 933: love means deeds, not sweet words.
