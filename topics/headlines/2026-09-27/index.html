Trump rejects Iran’s seven-day plan to reopen Hormuz

26 Sep 2026

BBC: President Trump said Tehran offered a deal to open the Strait of Hormuz immediately because it is “losing so badly,” and he rejected it. Iran’s foreign minister had said agreement was possible under a June MOU if conditions were met. About a fifth of world oil and gas usually moves through the waterway, effectively closed since the February war. Trump claimed the US already has “total control” of the strait with oil still moving, and that any acceptable deal would look different.

Nor’easter: NY and NJ emergencies as coastal flooding hits

26 Sep 2026

BBC / NWS: A powerful nor’easter peaked Saturday from Virginia to Maine with coastal flooding, strong winds, and heavy rain. New York and New Jersey declared states of emergency. Nearly 150,000 households lost power; more than 1,100 flights delayed and about 400 grounded. A full moon worsened coastal inundation in places such as Surf City and Manasquan, New Jersey.
