19 Sep 2026

India Today: Voting began Friday in Russia’s first parliamentary election since the full-scale invasion, with online casting and a weekend stretch aimed at the 111 million eligible. ABCD study: bedroom and mealtime screens tied to more teen cyberbullying

2026 (Journal of Adolescent Health)

PubMed / J Adolesc Health: Among 9,686 early adolescents in the US Adolescent Brain Cognitive Development cohort, bedroom, mealtime, and behaviour-management screen use correlated with higher cyberbullying victimization; limiting screen time correlated with lower victimization (cross-sectional — not proof of cause). Takeaway for parents of 9–14s: keep phones out of bedrooms and off the dinner table, and treat “limits” as a protection tool beside school policy.
