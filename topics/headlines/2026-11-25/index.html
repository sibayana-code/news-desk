Sandiganbayan · Marcoleta plunder case bail ruling

25 Nov 2026

  1. Sandiganbayan Third Division denied bail to Sen. Rodante Marcoleta and three co-accused in a plunder case.
  2. Co-accused are former congressman Mike Defensor and businessmen Aristotle Viray and Joseph Espiritu.
  3. Associate Justice Ronald Moreno penned the decision. The court held that the evidence of guilt is strong.
  4. The case covers P75 million that Marcoleta received while he was SAGIP party-list representative.
  5. P75 million exceeds the P50-million plunder threshold, so the charge is nonbailable.
  6. The court found that Marcoleta called the donations campaign funds but treated them as personal funds before Comelec.
  7. Status: all four accused remain in jail. Next checkpoint: trial proceedings at the Sandiganbayan, date not stated in the report.
