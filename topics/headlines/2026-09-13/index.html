Saudi pipeline shut as Houthis hold Bab el-Mandeb

12–13 Sep 2026

Reuters / Al-Monitor: Riyadh temporarily closed the 1,200-km East-West pipeline after drone hits in the Riyadh and Medina regions that Baghdad and Riyadh both traced to Iraq; Iraq sacked a Maysan commander Saturday. The line had been moving 4–5 million barrels a day — about 4–5% of global supply — while Hormuz stayed largely shut. Four Yemeni government sources say Houthis seized Perim (Mayun) Friday at Bab el-Mandeb; Houthi messaging keeps international shipping “safe” except Saudi vessels. MBS asked Trump for military help; Washington so far offers intel, not direct strikes.

Ukraine: $10B strike damage, hard winter warning

12–13 Sep 2026

Reuters via Straits Times / Star: Economy Minister Oleksandr Kravchenko put infrastructure and fixed-asset damage from Russian air strikes near $10 billion this year, with broader costs — including a de facto port blockade — around 1.5 points of GDP and ~$40 billion in export revenue at risk. Budget committee head Roksolana Pidlasa: domestic revenue underperformed by $1.35 billion in eight months; defence spent ~$42 billion while domestic take was ~$39 billion. Saturday strikes killed six and hit steel plants and shipping, Moscow said.

Kyiv preparing for October three-sided talks

12–13 Sep 2026

Reuters: Zelenskyy’s chief of staff Kyrylo Budanov said Ukraine is preparing for a possible October resumption of three-sided talks with Russia and the United States, days after US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner visited Moscow and Kyiv. Last such meeting was Geneva in February; efforts stalled while Washington focused on Iran. Peskov: Moscow does not rule talks out but says timing and venue are unsettled.
