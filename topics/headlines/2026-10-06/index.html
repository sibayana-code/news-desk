Yemen: coalition says it has retaken towns around Bab el-Mandeb

5–6 Oct 2026

AP via PBS / ABC: Saudi-led coalition forces say they recaptured several areas around the Bab el-Mandeb Strait from the Houthis, including the town of Dhubab, and coalition spokesman Turki al-Malki said more than 100 jets had secured “air protection” of the strait. President al-Alimi called the operation “Yemen Dawn” and named Sanaa as the goal. The Houthis had taken Mokha and the Yemeni side of the strait in September; about 12% of global trade passes through it. WHO counts at least 959 dead in this flare-up.

Brazil: Flávio Bolsonaro edges Lula, runoff set for Oct 25

4–5 Oct 2026

AP via PBS / Reuters: With 99.8% counted, Bolsonaro had just over 56 million votes (about 47%) to Lula’s 53.7 million (about 45%), so neither cleared 50% and the two meet again on Oct 25. The third- to fifth-place finishers are all on the right. Reuters says Brazilian stocks, bonds and the real were set to rally on the result.

Lancet review: addiction-like screen use tied to poorer youth mental health

4–5 Oct 2026

The Lancet / EurekAlert: An umbrella review of 121 reviews (about 9 million people under 25) links problematic, compulsive digital-media use to more anxiety and depression, loneliness, poor sleep and self-harm. The authors say screen-time limits alone are not enough and call for platform design rules.

Operational register. AMLC testimony read. Banks and insurers next.

Impeachment · Day 33 AMLC testimony lists 34 STRs on Duterte and 30 on Carpio

5 Oct 2026

  1. Identifier: Senate impeachment court, Day 33; Vice President Sara Duterte; Manases Carpio; AMLC Executive Director Ronel Buenaventura; presiding officer Francis Escudero.
  2. Target: Article II tests whether declared wealth matches bank, insurance, and transaction records for Duterte and Carpio.
  3. Ruling: Escudero allowed the AMLC testimony. He held that AMLA confidentiality rules do not bar compliance with a lawful court order.
  4. Status: Buenaventura read a summary of 34 suspicious transaction reports on Duterte and 30 on Carpio from 2007 to 2025.
  5. Quantity: The summary also lists 373 covered transaction reports for Duterte and 363 for Carpio. Combined, the reported transactions total about ₱4.4 billion.
  6. Detail: Two 2019 reports on premium payments to Mercantile Insurance cite graft linked to the DPWH flood-control issue. A 2024 report cites alleged shabu importation.
  7. Basis·variance: The ₱4.4 billion summary figure differs from the ₱6.77 billion pre-hearing estimate. The reports did not state amounts for the two 2019 entries.
  8. Limits: A suspicious transaction report is filed by a bank or insurer and is not proof of wrongdoing. No DPWH project, contractor, or contract amount was named.
  9. Next checkpoint: Bank and insurance witnesses are scheduled from today, then a BIR witness on income and tax records.
