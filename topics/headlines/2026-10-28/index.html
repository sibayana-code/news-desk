  1. Identifier: Senate TV, a channel for Senate proceedings, under a memorandum of understanding with IBC-13.

Senate · Civil society role in the national budget

27 Oct 2026

  1. Identifier: Senate Bill 2576 (Bantay Budget Act) and Senate Bill 2577 (Grassroots Participatory Budgeting Act).
  2. Actor: Sen. JV Ejercito, Finance Committee chairman, filed both after a 2027 budget consultation with civil society groups.
  3. Method: SB 2576 sets at least one hearing day per chamber for accredited groups and opens bicameral records to the public.
  4. Method: SB 2577 requires two local consultation assemblies a year and a one-fourth CSO share on development councils.
  5. Status: Filed only. Committee hearing date: pending.
