Local (top PH politics)

Operational register. A Senate resolution on the Chinese ambassador, and the Palace position on it. Sandiganbayan suspends Sen. Marcoleta.

Senate · Resolution No. 739 urging persona non grata status for Chinese Ambassador Jing Quan

19 Oct 2026

  1. Identifier: Senate Resolution No. 739, filed by Sen. Erwin Tulfo on 19 Oct; Chinese Ambassador Jing Quan.
  2. Basis: On 15 Oct, Jing said China “may easily win” if it used force, and the DFA called that a thinly veiled threat.
  3. Target: The resolution urges the DFA to recommend that Jing be declared persona non grata.
  4. Method: DFA Secretary Theresa Lazaro summoned Jing on 19 Oct and called the remarks provocative and unacceptable.
  5. Basis·variance: The Palace said President Marcos would not want a persona non grata declaration against any foreign envoy.
  6. Status: The resolution is filed and not adopted; the DFA reserves the right to take further diplomatic action.
  7. Next checkpoint: The Senate plenary vote on the resolution.

Sandiganbayan · 90-day preventive suspension of Sen. Rodante Marcoleta in plunder cases

19 Oct 2026

  1. Identifier: Sen. Rodante Marcoleta; Sandiganbayan Third Division; resolution penned by Justice Karl Miranda, promulgated 19 Oct.
  2. Basis: The charges say Marcoleta received ₱75 million in undeclared campaign donations from Mike Defensor and Aristotle Viray.
  3. Method: The court ordered a 90-day suspension, effective on his receipt of the resolution, under the Plunder and Anti-Graft laws.
  4. Basis·variance: The court said suspension is mandatory once a valid Information is established, so no hearing is required.
  5. Status: The suspension lifts automatically after 90 days; on 12 Oct the court also suspended Sen. Jinggoy Estrada for 90 days.
  6. Next checkpoint: Marcoleta’s receipt of the resolution starts the 90-day period.
