Local (top PH politics)

Operational register. Bank records enter the trial. ₱190M in year-end balances claimed.

Impeachment · Day 34 bank witnesses authenticate Duterte and Carpio account records

6 Oct 2026

  1. Identifier: Senate impeachment court, Day 34; Vice President Sara Duterte; Manases Carpio; presiding officer Francis Escudero.
  2. Target: Article II tests whether bank balances match the assets declared in the couple's SALNs from 2022 to 2025.
  3. Status: The AMLC witness finished testifying. The prosecution then called LandBank branch heads Eunice Sumatra and Raquel dela Cerna.
  4. Ruling: Escudero held that subpoenaed bank records carry a rebuttable presumption of regularity. The defense had refused to stipulate their authenticity.
  5. Claim: The prosecution offered to prove 30 active accounts in eight banks with more than ₱190 million in year-end balances.
  6. Basis·variance: The ₱190 million sums four separate year-end snapshots. It is not one balance held on a single date.
  7. Quantity: For 2022, declared other personal properties total ₱13.325 million. Year-end bank balances cited for that year exceed ₱59 million.
  8. Limits: These figures are the prosecution's offer of proof. The defense has not yet presented its account of the balances.
  9. Next checkpoint: About 20 bank and insurance witnesses remain, then a BIR witness on income and tax records.
