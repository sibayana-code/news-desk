Senate · Removal of unvetted DPWH projects from the 2027 General Appropriations Bill

2 Nov 2026

  1. Identifier: The House-transmitted 2027 General Appropriations Bill lists 16,244 DPWH projects, up from 11,420 in the executive proposal.
  2. Basis: House members added 6,271 projects that had no initial executive review, per Sen. Panfilo Lacson’s office.
  3. Actor: Finance Committee chair Sen. JV Ejercito set a DPWH deadline to submit supporting documents for those projects.
  4. Deadline: DPWH must submit complete documents by 3 Nov 2026, or the projects are removed before plenary.
  5. Scope: Lacson said the review covers Rep. Sandro Marcos’s district, which holds 405 added projects worth ₱4.37 billion.
  6. Method: Civil society groups will observe the bicameral conference committee, and its proceedings will be livestreamed.
  7. Next checkpoint: Plenary sessions resume 9 Nov 2026, and full budget debates start 23 Nov 2026.
