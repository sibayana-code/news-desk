Local (top PH politics)

Operational register. Day 39: three articles withdrawn; only unexplained wealth remains.

Impeachment · Prosecution drops Articles I, III and IV; court keeps Article II on unexplained wealth

13 Oct 2026

  1. Identifier: Senate impeachment court, Day 39; Vice President Sara Duterte; House lead prosecutor Rep. Gerville Luistro.
  2. Status: Luistro manifested that the prosecution will no longer pursue Article I (confidential funds), Article III (bribery) and Article IV (grave threats).
  3. Target: The case proceeds only on Article II — alleged unexplained wealth, SALN failures and prohibited business interests.
  4. Basis: Luistro said evidence already presented on Article II is enough to seek conviction and removal.
  5. Evidence: Prior testimony put alleged unexplained wealth near ₱817.87 million for Duterte and Manases Carpio, 2022–2025.
  6. Status: Presiding Officer Francis Escudero accepted the withdrawal; Articles I, III and IV will not go to a verdict.
  7. Limit: Escudero lifted sub judice limits on the withdrawn articles; Article II public-comment limits remain.
  8. Status: Defense counsel called the late withdrawal unfair after evidence had already been aired in public.
  9. Next checkpoint: Trial resumes 9 Nov 2026; defense presentation is expected when sessions restart.
