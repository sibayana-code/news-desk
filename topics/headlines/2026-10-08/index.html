Impeachment · Day 35 BPI witness traces ₱96 million in unused manager’s checks

7 Oct 2026

  1. Identifier: Senate impeachment court, Day 35; Vice President Sara Duterte; former President Rodrigo Duterte; BPI division head Marwin Galvez.
  2. Target: Article II tests whether the Vice President’s declared assets match her bank records and business interests.
  3. Evidence: Galvez testified that a father-and-daughter joint account bought two sets of unused manager’s checks worth ₱41 million and ₱55 million.
  4. Method: A manager’s check moves funds out of the client account into a bank settlement account, so year-end balances do not show them.
  5. Claim: The prosecution says the ₱96 million total shows a pattern of concealment in year-end balances.
  6. Evidence: Senator-judge Sherwin Gatchalian said the time deposits do not appear in the 2010 and 2011 SALNs he reviewed.
  7. Evidence: ₱16 million of the checks went to Sammy Uy, a man former Senator Antonio Trillanes IV has tagged as a drug personality.
  8. Evidence: Security Bank’s Leslie Cham said two Cale88 Foods accounts linked to Manases Carpio closed in July 2026.
  9. Quantity: BPI holds nine accounts of the couple, with a combined ending balance of ₱8 million as of 2025.
  10. Status: Defense counsel Michael Poa said the Vice President took no part in the joint-account transactions. That position is not yet tested.
  11. Next checkpoint: Insurance company witnesses follow the bank witnesses, starting with chief legal officer Juan Sotero Roman.
