20 Nov 2026

  1. Item: Senate impeachment court, trial of Vice President Sara Duterte on Article II, unexplained wealth. Day 45 was held on 20 Nov.
  2. Witness 1: The defense presented DTI Undersecretary Ceferino Rodolfo, managing head of the Board of Investments.
  3. Testimony 1: Rodolfo issued the export enterprise registration of Cale88, a firm linked to Manases Carpio. He said the BOI found no issue with its China exports.
  4. Headcount: Rodolfo said Cale88 had more than 100 employees in 2023 and 2024. This figure conflicts with records presented earlier in the trial.

  6. Testimony 2: Pagayunan said the first Cale88 application was denied for inconsistent ingredient declarations.
  7. Cross-examination: A defense witness admitted never visiting the Cale88 plant. Prosecutors questioned the relevance of both testimonies.
