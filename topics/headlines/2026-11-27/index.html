Operational register. Senate moves ₱19.8B from cash aid to PhilHealth.

Senate · 2027 budget, PhilHealth subsidy reallocation

27 Nov 2026

  1. The Senate finance committee cut ₱19.8 billion from two cash aid programs in the proposed 2027 budget.
  2. Cuts: ₱9.9 billion from AICS and ₱9.9 billion from the Medical Assistance Program.
  3. Reallocation: PhilHealth subsidy rises to ₱94.2 billion from ₱73 billion in the House version.
  4. Target basis: the committee sought ₱100 billion for PhilHealth; fiscal space limited the increase.
  5. Added lines: zero-balance billing, immunization, health promotion and provincial hospital upgrades.
  6. Schedule: plenary interpellation targets completion 27 Nov; second reading 3 Dec; third reading 7 Dec.
  7. Status: Senate version pending approval. Next checkpoint: bicameral conference 10 to 12 Dec.
