29 Oct 2026

  1. Identifier: Petition for certiorari and prohibition by Sens. Robin Padilla and Bong Go, with PDP-Laban.
  2. Target: The Senate impeachment court ruling of 23 Sep, voted 13-1, on how to count the two-thirds needed to convict.
  3. Basis: That ruling drops detained, suspended or incapacitated senators from the base, so fewer than 16 votes could convict.
  4. Status: A 14 Oct Supreme Court resolution gives the Senate and House five non-extendible days to comment.
