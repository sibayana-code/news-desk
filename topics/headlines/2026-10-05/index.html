Yemen’s government launches operation to retake Houthi territory

4 Oct 2026

BBC: President Rashad al-Alimi announced military operations to retake “the remaining territory of the republic” from the Iran-backed Houthis, with the front focused around Taiz. The same day the Houthis claimed missile and drone strikes on Aramco sites in Riyadh and Khurais, and Saudi strikes on Houthi targets continued. The war reignited in July; the Houthis are blockading Saudi energy exports already squeezed by Iran’s Hormuz closure.

Brazil count: Flávio Bolsonaro ahead of Lula in early returns

4–5 Oct 2026

Rio Times / Valor: With about 17% of polling stations counted, Flávio Bolsonaro had about 51% to Lula’s about 41%; the first TSE partial (1.7% counted) read 49.2% to 42.2%, with Augusto Cury near 3%. The count was still running at press time. Anything under 50% of valid votes sends the top two to an Oct 25 runoff; check the TSE page for the final first-round tally.

Merz arrives in Kyiv under sirens, pledges over €1.3B in aid

4 Oct 2026

NBC News / Kyiv Independent: German Chancellor Friedrich Merz made an unannounced visit to Kyiv as air-raid sirens sounded and a Russian drone hit the Northern Bridge. He met Zelenskyy and announced about €1 billion in military support, including interceptor drones and IRIS-T missiles, plus about €350 million for energy repairs, and advanced joint drone production. Zelenskyy told Reuters Ukraine will hit Russian refineries in response to Moscow’s stepped-up air campaign.

Catalonia floods kill two, one missing

4 Oct 2026

Reuters (via Devdiscourse): Heavy rain and flooding in Spain’s Catalonia left two dead and one missing on Sunday. Catalan leader Salvador Illa confirmed a man died when a wall collapsed in Tordera, north of Barcelona. Separately, tens of thousands marched across Spain over the housing crisis.

DepEd probes deaths of two pupils electrocuted at Isabela school fun run

3–5 Oct 2026

Manila Times / Philstar: Four pupils were electrocuted during a “bubbles” fun run at Cauayan South Central School on Oct 3; an 8-year-old and a 7-year-old died and two are in hospital. DepEd opened a fact-finding probe into the activity, safety measures, and equipment, and police are checking possible negligence by the organizer and bubble-machine operator, including an exposed extension cord. Parent ops: ask how school events power wet-play machines.

No in-person classes in Laguna public schools today for Teachers’ Day

4–5 Oct 2026

Inquirer / GMA: Several LGUs suspended face-to-face classes on Oct 5, National Teachers’ Day (RA 10743), including Laguna province public schools. DepEd says posts claiming a nationwide Oct 5–6 suspension are fake, so private schools and other areas follow their own announcements.

Study: owning a smartphone at 12 linked to higher disordered-eating risk

1 Oct 2026

Washington Post: A new study ties smartphone ownership by age 12 to a higher later risk of disordered eating. It lands alongside a UK cohort tying more than three hours a day of social media at ages 11–12 to later depression and anxiety, partly through lost sleep.

Asiad wrap: PH 4-9-16, biggest haul in six decades

4–5 Oct 2026

Rappler / ESPN: The Philippines closed Aichi-Nagoya with 29 medals, the same four golds as Hangzhou but up from 18 total. Operational register. AMLC witness today. Verdict estimate moves to November.

Impeachment · AMLC testimony opens week of bank and tax records; Gatchalian sees November verdict

4–5 Oct 2026

  1. Identifier: Senate impeachment court; Vice President Sara Duterte; Manases Carpio; AMLC Executive Director Ronel Buenaventura; presiding officer Francis Escudero; Senate President Sherwin Gatchalian.
  2. Target: Article II tests whether declared SALN wealth matches transaction, bank, insurance, and tax records for Duterte, Carpio, and linked firms.
  3. Status: Buenaventura takes the stand today at 10:00 on covered and suspicious transactions reported at about ₱6.77 billion. Escudero moved the testimony from Oct 1 so the defense could review AMLC summaries and 23 to 24 document boxes.
  4. Method: Full-day hearings run Oct 5 to 9. Seven banks and seven insurance or investment firms testify from Tuesday, then a BIR witness presents income and tax records.
  5. Basis·variance: Gatchalian estimates 30 to 45 more days, which puts a verdict in mid to late November. The court spokesman’s earlier Dec 16 date is now superseded by this estimate.
  6. Limits: The ₱6.77 billion figure counts reported transactions, not proven unexplained wealth. The November date is an estimate and depends on a late-October break, Nov 9 budget debates, and the Nov 16 to 18 ASEAN Summit.
  7. Evidence: GMA, Rappler, and Manila Times on the AMLC schedule; BusinessMirror on the week plan; Philstar 5 Oct on the Gatchalian estimate.
