OSG · Advice on 2026 funds covered by the Supreme Court budget ruling

22 Oct 2026

  1. Identifier: Solicitor General Darlene Berberabe; Special Provisions 1(a) and 1(b), Chapter XLIII, 2026 General Appropriations Act.
  2. Basis: The Supreme Court declared those provisions unconstitutional; they allowed unprogrammed funds from excess or new revenue.
  3. Status: Berberabe advised the DBM and the executive to hold off on using the affected funds for now.
  4. Method: The OSG will file a motion for clarification and partial reconsideration that raises judicial overreach.
  5. Evidence: Budget Secretary Kim Robert De Leon said no 2026 funds were charged against 1(a) or 1(b).
  6. Next checkpoint: OSG appeal deadline of 4 Nov 2026, per Philstar.
