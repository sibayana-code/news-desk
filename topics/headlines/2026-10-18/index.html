Ombudsman · Plunder and graft probe of Cagayan Rep. Joseph Lara and relatives over DPWH contracts

17–18 Oct 2026

  1. Identifier: Cagayan Rep. Joseph Lara, his wife, four sons and a daughter-in-law; Ombudsman Jesus Crispin Remulla.
  2. Target: Pulsar, JDL5 and JLL Pulsar construction firms, which won DPWH infrastructure contracts while Lara held office.
  3. Basis: Remulla said the claimed divestment did not happen because contract money still reached Lara’s private bank account.
  4. Evidence: An AMLC report shows Lara received ₱1.9 billion from relatives, affiliates and DPWH offices between 2019 and 2025.
  5. Basis·variance: The complaint puts alleged ill-gotten wealth at about ₱10.02 billion; this figure is an allegation, not validated.
  6. Status: The case is at preliminary investigation, and Lara’s office denies wrongdoing.
  7. Next checkpoint: The respondents file answers, and the Ombudsman decides whether to file charges in court.
