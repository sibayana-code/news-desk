Senate · Sandiganbayan suspension orders for Sens. Jinggoy Estrada and Rodante Marcoleta

25 Oct 2026

  1. Identifier: Sens. Jinggoy Estrada and Rodante Marcoleta; 90-day preventive suspensions ordered by the Sandiganbayan.
  2. Basis: Estrada faces plunder and graft charges over flood control projects. Marcoleta faces plunder over ₱75 million in undeclared campaign contributions.
  3. Status: Senate President Sherwin Gatchalian confirmed receipt of the orders and holds both in abeyance.
  4. Method: Both senators said they will file motions for reconsideration, which defer a suspension until resolved.
  5. Limit: Estrada, Marcoleta and Sen. Loren Legarda receive no salary because of absences from office.
  6. Next checkpoint: Filing and resolution of the two motions for reconsideration. Charges against Legarda: pending, no timeline.

Impeachment court · Presiding officer position on a vice presidential vacancy

25 Oct 2026

  1. Identifier: Sen. Francis Escudero, presiding officer in the impeachment trial of Vice President Sara Duterte.
  2. Status: Escudero said he would decline the vice presidency if Duterte is removed, citing conflict of interest.
  3. Basis: The President nominates a replacement from Congress, confirmed by a majority of each chamber voting separately.
  4. Limit: Escudero said no offer exists, and his position does not bind other senator-judges.
  5. Evidence: One article remains, on unexplained wealth. Conviction needs two-thirds, or 14 votes if 20 senators qualify.
  6. Next checkpoint: Trial resumes on 9 Nov, with the defense still to present evidence.
