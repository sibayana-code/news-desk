Operational register. Senate takes the P7.2-trillion 2027 budget to plenary debate.

Senate · Plenary debate on the 2027 General Appropriations Bill

24 Nov 2026

  1. Item: Senate version of the 2027 General Appropriations Bill, P7.2 trillion, submitted to plenary by the finance committee.
  2. Status: Senate plenary debates on the budget bill begin this week, per Philstar on 24 Nov 2026.
  3. Basis: Finance chair JV Ejercito said the Senate version prioritizes food, health, education, employment and security.
  4. Health: The Department of Health and attached agencies receive P378.6 billion in the Senate version.
  5. PhilHealth: The subsidy rises to P94.2 billion from P74.4 billion in the House version, to support zero-balance billing.
  6. Medical Assistance Program: The Senate cut the allocation to P16.2 billion, which is P32 billion below the House version.
  7. Public works: DPWH receives P587 billion, with completion of ongoing projects set as the priority after the flood control issue.
  8. Next checkpoint: Floor debate and amendments in plenary; final passage date is not stated in the report.
