  3. Limit: Records before 30 June 2022 may establish only a factual baseline for assets during 2022 to 2025 and may not prove separate prior offenses.
  4. Excluded: Commission on Audit reports on confidential-fund disbursements in the Office of the Vice President and the Department of Education were excluded.
  5. Evidence: AMLC intelligence reports and covered and suspicious transaction reports involving the Vice President, Manases Carpio and associated businesses were admitted.
  6. Status: Next checkpoint: continuation of the impeachment trial calendar under the admitted baseline parameters.
