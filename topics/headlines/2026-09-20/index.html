Trump signs sweeping Russia–Iran sanctions law; oil-buyer tariffs authorized

19 Sep 2026

Al Jazeera / AFP / Reuters: President Donald Trump signed the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026 on Friday — the first major Ukraine-related bill through Congress in more than two years. It targets Putin, oligarchs, banks, energy, defence, and the shadow tanker fleet, and lets the White House impose tariffs of up to 100% on the five largest buyers of Russian oil and gas (China and India most exposed). Zelenskyy called it “an extremely powerful tool”; India restated energy-security priorities ahead of a Xi visit next week.

PISA 2025: weekly school talk with parents slips to 54%; problem talk to 38%

11 Sep 2026 (live desk)

Daily Tribune / DepEd: Education Secretary Sonny Angara flagged PISA 2025 figures showing 54% of Filipino learners discuss what they did at school with parents at least weekly (down from 57% in 2022), and only 38% talk weekly about school problems (down from 45%). Global peer systems also slipped. Practical ask for ages 7–teens: one short daily check-in on the school day and on stuck spots — the same habit DepEd is pushing alongside Kaagapay parent sessions through November.
