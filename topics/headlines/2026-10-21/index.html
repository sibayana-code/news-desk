20 Oct 2026

  1. Identifier: Senate resolution filed by Sen. Risa Hontiveros on 20 Oct; Chinese Ambassador Jing Quan.
  2. Target: The resolution urges President Marcos to declare Jing persona non grata or to ask Beijing to recall him.
  3. Basis: It follows Resolution No. 739 by Sen. Erwin Tulfo over Jing’s 15 Oct remark that China “may easily win” by force.
  4. Method: Nine House members, including Rep. Leila de Lima, issued a joint statement asking the President to reconsider expulsion.
  5. Basis·variance: The Palace said any apology should be voluntary and left the matter to DFA Secretary Theresa Lazaro.
  6. Status: The Chinese Embassy said the lawmakers who seek expulsion are no longer welcome in China.
  7. Next checkpoint: The Palace update on the DFA meeting with Jing, and Senate action on both resolutions.
