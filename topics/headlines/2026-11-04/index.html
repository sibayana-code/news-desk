Operational register. Senate removes 295 DPWH projects worth ₱8.836 billion from the 2027 budget for missing documents.

Senate · Removal of non-compliant DPWH projects from the proposed 2027 budget

3 Nov 2026

  1. Identifier: Senate President Sherwin Gatchalian announced removal of 295 DPWH infrastructure projects worth ₱8.836 billion.
  2. Basis: A letter from former DPWH Secretary Vince Dizon listed the 295 projects as non-compliant in the agency evaluation.
  3. Requirement: DPWH Secretary Giovanni Lopez said each project needs five documents before it can be funded or implemented.
  4. Documents: The five are a program of work, geotagged photos, BP202 form, certificate of implementability and LGU no-objection.
  5. Scope: Gatchalian said 7,107 of 11,395 projects reviewed, or more than 62%, carry at least one major red flag.
  6. Deadline: The Finance Committee deadline for documents on 6,271 House-added projects was 3 Nov 2026.
  7. Status: Removal applies before plenary deliberations. Next checkpoint: Senate plenary debate on the 2027 budget bill.
