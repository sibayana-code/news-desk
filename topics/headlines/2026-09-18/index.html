PISA 2025: 47% of Filipino 15-year-olds report recurring bullying

9 Sep 2026

BusinessWorld / OECD: Nearly half of Philippine 15-year-olds (47%) said they were bullied at least several times a month in 2025 — more than double the OECD average of 20%, and up from 2022. About 14% reported upsetting information posted about them online without consent a few times a month (OECD average 3%). Sample: 8,072 students from 208 schools. The print lands beside DepEd Order 006’s tougher penalties — the enforcement gap is still the live school question.
