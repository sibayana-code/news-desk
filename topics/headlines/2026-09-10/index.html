Raising kids

DepEd Order 006 tightens bullying and campus-violence penalties. Nationwide active-shooter drills ran under a trauma-informed “Do No Harm” rewrite.

DepEd: severe bullying can mean exclusion

10 Sep 2026

Under DepEd Order No. 006, s. 2026 (ESMLE), repeated bullying/online harassment can hit five-day suspension plus DSWD referral, then non-readmission, then exclusion. High-risk acts (severe harm, bomb threats, dangerous hoaxes) jump to non-readmission or permanent exclusion and police handoff. Child Protection Committees must investigate, protect victims, and open mental-health support. Parent cue: know your school’s handbook ladder before the first incident report.

4 Sep 2026

The National Simultaneous School Safety Drill (reset to 3 Sep after mental-health pushback) banned hyper-realistic role-play and surprise runs.
