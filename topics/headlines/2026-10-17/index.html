Senate · Senate leaders reject the claim that the 2027 budget is leverage over senator-judges

15–16 Oct 2026

  1. Identifier: Senate President Sherwin Gatchalian; Senate President Pro Tempore Vicente Sotto III; Senators Erwin Tulfo and Panfilo Lacson.
  2. Basis: Vice President Sara Duterte alleged on 14 Oct that the budget is a bargaining chip in her impeachment trial.
  3. Evidence: Duterte cited cash distribution and political maneuvering but named no transaction or person.
  4. Status: Gatchalian said the budget has no link to the trial judgment, and Sotto said the Senate will not allow that use.
  5. Method: Gatchalian said hearing transcripts, committee reports, the third-reading version and amendments will be public.
  6. Next checkpoint: Senate deliberation on the ₱7.2 trillion bill continues; the impeachment trial resumes 9 Nov 2026.

Senate Finance · Senators move to restore funding for the DepEd ARAL tutoring program

16 Oct 2026

  1. Identifier: Academic Recovery and Accessible Learning (ARAL) program under Republic Act 12028; Senate Committee on Finance.
  2. Target: DepEd says ₱15.68 billion is needed to run ARAL in 2027.
  3. Miss: The executive proposal gave ARAL zero, and the House version gives ₱350 million, about 2% of the need.
  4. Basis: DepEd reports struggling readers fell from 6.7 million to 2.2 million in the program’s first year.
  5. Status: Gatchalian and Senators Bam Aquino, Joel Villanueva and Erwin Tulfo raised the zero allocation; Gatchalian said he will push to restore it.
  6. Next checkpoint: Senate committee report on the DepEd budget; DepEd is asking for ₱147.9 billion more in total.
