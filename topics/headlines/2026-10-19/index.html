Operational register. House prosecutors send dropped impeachment evidence on VP Duterte to the Ombudsman. The Senate weighs a resolution on the Chinese ambassador.

House prosecutors · Turnover of evidence from three dropped impeachment articles against VP Sara Duterte to the Ombudsman

18–19 Oct 2026

  1. Identifier: Vice President Sara Duterte; Manila 3rd district Rep. Joel Chua, House prosecutor; Ombudsman Jesus Crispin Remulla.
  2. Target: Documentary and testimonial evidence on the three dropped articles, which cover confidential funds, grave threats and direct bribery of education officials.
  3. Method: House prosecutors will turn the evidence over to the Ombudsman, as Remulla requested.
  4. Basis: Confidential funds of the Office of the Vice President and DepEd total ₱612.5 million, which is above the ₱50 million plunder threshold.
  5. Basis·variance: Chua named plunder and malversation as possible charges; no criminal case is filed, so the charges are pending.
  6. Status: The three articles are out of the impeachment case, and their criminal review moves to the Ombudsman.
  7. Next checkpoint: The Ombudsman receives the evidence and decides whether to open a preliminary investigation.
