Senate Finance Committee · DPWH document deadline for House-added 2027 budget projects

3 Nov 2026

  1. Identifier: The House 2027 General Appropriations Bill lists 6,271 DPWH projects that were not in the executive proposal.
  2. Deadline: DPWH must submit complete documents for those projects to the Senate Finance Committee by 3 Nov 2026.
  3. Requirement: Each project needs a certificate of implementability, a program of works, development council approval and geotagged photos.
  4. Miss: Sen. JV Ejercito said a project missing any requirement is removed, whether it came from DPWH, the House or the Senate.
  5. Method: Eight Finance Committee staff and a separate team under Senate President Vicente Sotto review the projects line by line.
  6. Scope: Watchdogs flagged Ilocos Norte District 1, which rose from ₱369 million in the executive proposal to about ₱4 billion.
