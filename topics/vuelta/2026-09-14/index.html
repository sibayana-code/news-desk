Johannessen takes Granada. Mas seals Spain’s first since Contador.

Facts

Stage 21, Sun 13 Sep: Carrefour Granada → Granada, 112 km (~1,300 m), Alto de la Alhambra circuits. Winner Tobias Halland Johannessen (Uno-X Mobility) 2:54:08; 2. Alessandro Romele; 3. Ramses Debruyne; 4. Matthew Brennan; 5. Bastien Tronchon. GC times frozen with 9.8 km left (final lap neutralised for GC after CPA/ASO talks). No major DNFs flagged on the last day.

Final GC: red Enric Mas (Movistar) 73:52:55; Roglič +2:15; Gall +2:44; Carapaz +6:54; Kuss +8:45. Green van Aert (326). Polka Buitrago (78). White Onley (first British). Teams: Decathlon CMA CGM. Race over — no tomorrow.

Tale of the tape

Procession, champagne, then the Alhambra bites. Bilbao goes long on his last Grand Tour stage; Laurance, Brennan, and a reshuffled dozen fight the circuits. Into the last kilometre Debruyne, Johannessen, and Romele open the gap. Johannessen leads the corner and holds Romele — first Grand Tour stage win, one day after Alguacil silver.

Behind: Mas rolls in with six Movistar teammates. Spain’s first Vuelta since Contador 2014. Movistar’s first Grand Tour since 2019. Pogacar crashed out on stage 8; Mas took the red and never gave it back. Visma finish the race on seven stage wins (Brennan five, van Aert two) but leave Granada without a sixth for the Brit.
