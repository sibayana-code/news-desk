Facts

Rest day 1, Mon 31 Aug. No racing. Jerseys unchanged from Stage 9 (Alto de Aitana): red Enric Mas (Movistar) 28:34:03; Roglič +1:18; Gall +1:39; Onley +2:20 (white); Carapaz +3:26. Green van Aert. Polka Buitrago. Team Decathlon CMA CGM.

Tomorrow Stage 10, Tue 1 Sep: Alcaraz → Elche de la Sierra, 184.7 km, hilly, three cat. 3s (~2,991 m), intermediate Hellín km 125, last climb Puerto de Socovos 9.7 km at 3.5% (summit 21.5 km out), rising finish. Next after that: Stage 11 Cartagena → Lorca (flat), then Calar Alto on Stage 12.

Tale of the tape

Monday was the first pause after Aitana cooked the first week. Mas finally celebrated in red after refusing the jersey on Saturday’s podium. Roglič is still inside 80 seconds. Onley is the white-jersey problem nobody wants to ignore. Pogačar’s week ended in surgery, not a hospital meme.

Tuesday is not a GC day on paper. Nearly 3,000 m of rolling climb, no summit finish, rest-day legs. Someone has to chase. If Visma, Lidl-Trek and friends look at each other, the break wins. If they cooperate, van Aert / Pedersen / Brennan / Coquard get a messy uphill sprint.
