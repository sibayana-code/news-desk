Tale of the tape

Rest-day legs, breakaway bait, and Visma still did the housework for van Aert. Seven up the road, gap over 2:30, reeled inside 5 km. Brennan burned the last chase on Calzoni. Then Fedorov and Azparren jumped with a kilometre left. Caught on the line. Tronchon came from the mess, beat Cort and Nys, and did not even throw his hands up because he thought someone was still ahead.

Then the tears. First Grand Tour stage for a Frenchman who finished outside the top 100 on Aitana and told Guillaume Martin to go full gas until he died. Van Aert fourth. Green padded. Red untouched. Week two just got a plot that nobody had on the bingo card.
