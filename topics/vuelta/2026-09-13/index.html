Landa resurrects on Alguacil. Mas all but home.

Facts

Stage 20, Sat 12 Sep: La Calahorra → Collado del Alguacil, 186.8 km (~4,900 m climbing), HC summit (8.3 km @ 9.8%, ramps to 22%). Winner Mikel Landa (Soudal Quick-Step) 4:58:01; 2. Tobias Johannessen +0:47; 3. Ramses Debruyne +1:27; 4. Pau Miquel +3:00; 5. Urko Berrade +4:12. GC: Gall +6:37 on the stage (17″ ahead of Mas); Roglič +7:32; Carapaz +8:31; Kuss 8th on stage +4:35. No major GC DNFs flagged.

GC: red Mas 70:56:58; Roglič +2:15; Gall +2:44; Carapaz +6:54; Kuss +8:45 (up from 11th); Onley +9:28 (white, +1:10 on Omrzel). Green van Aert sealed on the Purche sprints. Polka Buitrago. Tomorrow Stage 21: Carrefour Granada → Granada, 112 km processional — Alhambra parade, Paseo del Salón finish. Stay upright and Spain has its first Vuelta winner since Contador 2014.

Tale of the tape

Queen stage, breakaway carnival. Van Aert lights the flag; Uno-X floods the front; Kuss and Carapaz go long with ~100 km still to race. Landa bridges, then dumps Johannessen and Debruyne with 7 km of Alguacil left — first Grand Tour stage win since 2015, after a year he called the worst of his career. Landismo, one more time.

Behind: Mas just counts kilometres. Gall nicks 17″ and closes to 29″ of Roglič for second, but the red jersey’s lead balloons to 2:15. Kuss’s all-day raid jumps him six places into fifth. One ceremonial lap left. Unless gravity invents a new law in Granada, this is Mas’s race.
