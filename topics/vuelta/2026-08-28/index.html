Pogačar beats Mas in Castelló

Alcossebre → Castelló, 177.4 km

28 Aug 2026

Medium mountains, gravel on Puerto El Bartolo. Pogačar dropped the race with 24 km left, Mas caught him at the summit, Pogačar nearly went off-road on the descent. Sprint à deux in Castelló: Pogačar 4:12:40, Mas same time, Debruyne +46. Third stage win of the race.

Open the piece

Tale of the tape

First real GC fight of the week. Mas was the only rider who could live with Pogačar on El Bartolo. They rode the last 10 km together into a headwind, then Pogačar had the kick. Mas jumps to second overall at 3:34. Roglič slips to third at 4:21.

Three wins in six stages, red from day 1. Not seen since Freire in 2007. Brennan disappeared out the back. Green still Pogačar, van Aert took the Benicàssim sprint and is now second on points.

Takes

  • Pogačar: “I was a little bit shocked when Enric passed me… I was sure he knew the downhill. Chapeau to him.” Quotes
  • Mas is the first rider to make Pogačar look mortal on this Vuelta. Tomorrow was Valdelinares, the first proper summit finish.
  • Tarling and Beloki DNF. White still Onley.
