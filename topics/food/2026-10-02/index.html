BSP: September inflation may hit a three-year high on weather food

1 Oct 2026

Philstar (Keisha Ta-Asan): Bangko Sentral says September headline inflation likely landed between 6.4% and 7.4%, up from August’s 6.1%. The upper end would be the fastest since March 2023’s 7.6%. Even the low end keeps inflation above the 2–4% target for a seventh straight month. PSA releases the official print Monday, Oct 6. BSP’s named upward pressures: weather-related lifts in vegetables, fish, rice, and fruit; higher domestic petroleum; peso depreciation that raises import costs for fuel, food, and raws. Named offsets: lower meat prices and electricity. RCBC’s Michael Ricafort centers 6.7% and warns a strong El Niño could keep pressing rice and other food into early 2027, with second-round effects via the ₱60 Metro Manila daily minimum-wage hike and energy/import pass-through. BSP has already lifted the policy rate 75bp to 5% since April. Operator read for Calamba: this is yesterday’s PSA kitchen receipt upgraded to a macro threat. Rice at ~₱56 and +19% YoY is not a one-week blip — it is the floor under every rice meal and every QSR value stack into Q4. Do not rewrite fixed lunch prices before Oct 6; do float highland/lowland veg specials and dual-source greens. Soft meat that “offset” September is the same soft pork DA wants to reverse with 2027 tariff steps — treat cheap protein as a hedge window, not a new baseline. If Ricafort’s second-round call lands, wage and logistics will reprice your ticket even if commodity prints ease.

Wall Street Wraps plants a Vista Mall Pampanga door

1 Oct 2026

Punto! Central Luzon: Wall Street Wraps opened at Vista Mall Pampanga on MacArthur Highway, San Agustin, City of San Fernando — ribbon with owners Sean Hayes and Aireen Acoba, franchisees Kim and April Pineda, Mayor Vilma Caluag. Format is scratch wraps, bowls, salads, and wellness drinks: Caesar Wrap and Burrito Bowl (whole-grain rice) as named favorites, plus Downtown Burrito, Thai Chili Wrap, Uptown Caesar; drink lane Morning Bell, Golden Bull (ginger/turmeric/pepper/lemon), Greenback (cucumber-mint). Prior footprint: AUF (opp. BPI), Plaridel (across La Grande), Clark Center at BerthaPhil Freeport — now a fourth Central Luzon node on a Vista mall. Operator read: this is not a Calamba rent bet. It is the mid-ticket healthy-fast lane still franchising into provincial malls while QSR value wars stay loud. Steal the architecture — bowl/wrap core + attach drinks that sell wellness without reinventing the kitchen — if you densify a Laguna lunch lane. Watch Clark → San Fernando as a catchment strip that pulls OFW and BPO traffic; if that lane keeps filling Vista boxes, the same format will test south of SLEX soon. FLAG: opening coverage is brand-friendly local desk; treat menu claims as promo until you taste the door.

FAO: PH reclaims #2 banana exporter — +30% to 3 MMT

26 Sep 2026 (2025 trade year)

BusinessMirror / FAO: The Philippines reclaimed the world’s second-largest banana-exporter rank in 2025 after shipments jumped 30% to 3 million metric tons from 2.33 MMT in 2024, reversing the slide that left Guatemala ahead after 2022. FAO credits better TR4 (Fusarium Wilt Tropical Race 4) management, easing civil unrest, and “substantial” production investments in Cagayan Valley — including DA farm inputs and fertilizers. China and Japan remain the key markets; PH still supplies 75–80% of Japan’s banana imports, though Japan’s take from Vietnam rose 38%. On China quotes, Vietnam averaged ~$407/MT versus ~$600 for Philippine and Cambodian fruit (Ecuador ~$755). FAO flags the usual export-risk stack: conflict, fertilizer/energy/transport shocks, high living costs and rates. Operator read: this is an agri-export win, not a cheaper local Cavendish plate for Calamba kitchens. What matters for an opener is the signal — Mindanao/Cagayan Valley supply recovering under disease discipline and DA input support. That same discipline is what you want on any protein or produce line you dual-source. Vietnam’s China price undercut is the competitive reminder: volume reclaim does not equal pricing power. For kitchen planning, keep bananas as garnish/dessert attach, not a cost thesis; for entrepreneurial read, watch which packers translate TR4 control into steady weekly export cadence — those are the operators worth knowing when you need reliable tropical fruit into a menu or a commissary.
