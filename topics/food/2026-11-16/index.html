Takes

  • Jollibee's export model is franchising: two state-exclusive partners in Australia commit the capital (an estimated A$60M) while JFC supplies the brand and the system. A local brand can copy this at a smaller scale by giving master franchises for regions outside your home base, such as Visayas or Mindanao, instead of opening company-owned stores there. Open
  • Jollibee's Christmas plan runs on three things: the store as a place to visit (300+ lit stores until 1 Jan), a ₱79 dessert made with a chef, and big group orders. Check you have each one: a reason for people to come in, a low-priced seasonal item they buy on impulse, and a party-order path with a cutoff date.
  • The ₱99 vs ₱199 post shows customers will do the math on your combos. Before December, check every meal tier so the bigger option never works out to worse value than buying two smaller ones.

Jollibee to open 35 restaurants in Australia

15 Nov 2026 · The Philippine Times

Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese, in Manila for the ASEAN summit, joined JFC Global President and CEO Ernesto Tanmantiong at Jollibee Biopolis in Pasay on Sunday 15 Nov to announce two exclusive franchise agreements signed that morning. Happy Food Group will bring Jollibee to Victoria and Lucky Bee Australia will take it to New South Wales. The partners plan 35 stores within five years, an estimated A$60M investment with about 900 direct jobs. Tanmantiong said Jollibee is now at nearly 2,000 stores in 17 countries.

Operator read: JFC is growing abroad on its partners' money, with exclusive rights for a whole state and a five-year opening schedule. The two partners carry the cost of building stores. JFC keeps control of the brand and earns from every store. The 400,000 Australians of Filipino heritage that Albanese mentioned give the first stores a ready customer base. For an opener here, the lessons are that exclusive territory is worth a lot to a franchisee if you attach a minimum opening schedule to it, and that a large diaspora lowers the risk of the first store when you go abroad.

Jollibee spreads Christmas joy across the Philippines

15 Nov 2026 · Corner Magazine PH · Flag: brand-led coverage

More than 300 Jollibee stores are lit for Christmas, open from 12 Nov to 1 Jan. This is the fifth year of the store-lighting events. On the menu side there's a ₱79 Pistachio Choco Sundae made with pastry chef Nouel Catis (until 31 Dec) and a ₱379 Jolly Charm meal that comes with a plush bag charm (3 Nov to 31 Dec). From 1 to 15 Dec, the ₱245 Super Meal A with Large Coke comes with a free Coca-Cola color-changing cup. Family Super Meals start at ₱499, and Christmas BIG Order handles large group orders.

Operator read: each part has a job. The lights get people to visit, the dessert sells on impulse, the collectible gets people coming back, the drink partner pays for the cup giveaway, and BIG Order captures party orders. You don't need a big budget to copy this: decorate one store well enough for photos, add one seasonal dessert under ₱100, and get a beverage supplier to pay for a small giveaway. Like the Coke cup, run the giveaway only during the payday weeks.
