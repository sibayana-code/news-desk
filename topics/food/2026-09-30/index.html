Durian + mangosteen trial hits Canada — same Vancouver pipe as mango

23–24 Sep 2026

DA: nearly one metric ton of fresh durian and mangosteen is on trial into Canada this week — Vancouver on Sep 23 (300 kg durian + 120 kg mangosteen) for Fresh Makers Ltd., then Toronto on Sep 24 (300 kg durian + 200 kg mangosteen). Buyer contact named: Jackson Ng. The fruits flew Philippine Airlines. Sec. Tiu Laurel ties the flights to the Marcos directive to open new farm markets and chip at an agri trade deficit above USD11 billion a year since 2022. Undersecretary Philip Young: market development has to become concrete offtake for farmers. Context that matters for operators: Fresh Makers is the same Vancouver distributor that just took Mensch FilAm’s one-ton Carabao mango commercial line (Sep 18) — so Canada is stacking tropical SKUs on one retail/institutional pipe, not running one-off PR tons. The exporter already completed three air-cargo durian shipments to France with weekly cadence continuing. Parallel BPI track (Philstar Sep 22): reciprocal UK access talks covering Cavendish banana, MD2 pineapple, Carabao mango, Solo papaya, Hass avocado, value-added purple yam and specialty coffee against UK fresh apples, with an audit visit floated for October. Operator read for Calamba: yesterday’s mango desk was the Canada door; today’s is the product-mix tell. When one distributor adds durian and mangosteen beside mango, peak-season Mindanao kilos get another bidder. If you run tropical desserts, beverages, or “local exotic” specials into Q4, lock volume and landed cost now — trial success plus a France weekly already means export premium can move with kitchen spike, same as the carabao mango print near ₱183/kg earlier this month.

El Niño’s first ₱6.87B is mostly corn — feed before rice theater

26–27 Sep 2026

DA’s Sep 26 damage bulletin (Context Sep 27): ongoing El Niño has already booked ₱6.87 billion in farm losses — 223,214 farmers, 249,028 hectares, 163,812 metric tons of production gone. Corn carries the print: ₱4.90 billion across 170,605 ha and 126,883 MT. Rice is second at ₱1.53 billion / 25,196 MT. High-value crops (vegetables, banana, pineapple) add ₱396.93 million; livestock and poultry ₱36.94 million. About 77% of affected land can still recover; 56,262 ha are written off. DA has already rolled ₱10.96 billion in interventions (₱10.53B seeds/fertilizer/inputs plus ₱427M insurance to 42,383 farmers), with cloud seeding, pumps, and native-chicken distribution on the side. These are initial estimates and will rise if the drought deepens into the PAGASA “very strong” window. Operator read: do not read this as another rice-headline day. Corn is feed. Feed is chicken, eggs, and anything else that clears your fryer or brunch plate. Yesterday’s desk already had USDA cutting the milled crop and DA wiring the ₱9.7B UPLIFT package — today’s bulletin is the production-side receipt, skewed hard to corn. For a Calamba kitchen, reprice poultry and egg contracts against this feed shock before Bantay Presyo moves the retail sticker. Dual-source greens stay on (HVC is in the damage column too), but the asymmetric risk is protein cost, not another palay speech.

llaollao lands Evo City Kawit — premium dessert on the Makro estate

~29 Sep 2026 (Homes.ph)

Homes.ph: Spanish frozen-yogurt chain llaollao opened at Ayala Malls Evo City in Kawit, Cavite — custom cups with tropical fruit, cookie crumble, Lotus Biscoff and pistachio toppings — another international lifestyle F&B pin on Ayala’s 200-ha Kawit estate. The mall’s main building is still targeted for Q4 2026; Makro’s Cavite return remains on the same estate lease slate for Q4 2026–Q1 2027 alongside Cloverleaf, Arca South, and Broadfield Biñan. Parallel Visayas coffee densify (same week’s Homes.ph tape): Pick Up Coffee’s 24-hour kiosk at Enterprise One, Iloilo Business Park (espresso ₱50–120), and Bo’s Coffee into Robinsons Dumaguete L2 Expansion (PH Arabica, 120+ domestic doors). Operator read for Calamba: llaollao is not your lunch competitor — it is the ASP and lease-comp signal. Every premium dessert brand that clears Kawit rent raises what landlords think south Luzon households will pay, and it sits on the same CALAX/CAVITEX corridor where you already watch Makro Broadfield as a wholesale option. Steal the catchment map (estate densifies dining + cash-and-carry together), not the yogurt format. If you are hunting a Calamba or Sto. Tomas door, price against Nuvali Central Bloc / SM Sto. Tomas weekday tickets — treat Evo City FOHO as proof the south belt keeps loading lifestyle brands before you use it to justify a rent that still needs a weekday cover.
