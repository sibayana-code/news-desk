Grab puts a verified Halal filter on Davao

9–12 Sep 2026

Grab Philippines and NCMF Region XI signed an MOU on Sep 9 in Davao to make Halal discovery real on GrabFood: search/filter for “halal” only returns merchants endorsed by NCMF and vetted by recognized certification bodies; the Philippine Halal logo sits on the merchant icon; Muslim-owned spots can add a separate tag. Grab is also stripping unsupported “halal” wording from restaurant names. Edge Davao put the live merchant count at 17 Halal/Muslim-owned on the platform in the city. Broader talks cover merchant education and consumer campaigns. COO/opener take: this is a platform trust product, not a promo. If you list on Grab (or plan a second brand that needs dietary clarity), the scrub of fake labels is the signal — accuracy becomes a ranking and visibility asset. Davao is the pilot; expect copycats in other Muslim-traffic cities once the audit playbook works.

Saba to HK and NZ — $291M fruit book at AFL

9 Sep 2026

DA: Philippine banana exporters signed contracts at Asia Fruit Logistica in Hong Kong (first PH return in a decade) to ship Cardaba (saba) to Hong Kong and New Zealand buyers. Exhibition fruit orders totaled $290.71 million — durian $206.23M, Cavendish $11M, Cardaba $3.08M, plus dragon fruit $1.02M and mangoes $170,800. Assistant Secretary Arnel De Mesa: demand is rising; the hard part is meeting volume and quality standards. Next pitch: FAO Hand-in-Hand Investment Forum in Rome in October (mango, seaweed, coffee, cacao). Sec. Tiu Laurel framed the push against an ~$11 billion annual agricultural trade deficit. Kitchen/opener read: this is export-side heat, not a retail price cut. Saba moving into more overseas contracts can tighten domestic cooking-banana supply in pockets — watch farmgate chatter if dessert and saba snacks are on your menu. The bigger signal is DA hunting high-value export lanes while the deficit stays huge.

Vista weighs ₱15B in mall sales for a 2027 bond

8 Sep 2026

Insider PH / CreditSights: Vista Land & Lifescapes is weighing the sale of about ₱15 billion in malls — a possible ₱10B non-core Mandaluyong asset CreditSights tags as likely Starmall EDSA-Shaw, plus a ~₱5B Southern Philippines property — to raise cash ahead of a $420 million bond due July 2027 (about ₱26.3B at ₱62.70/$). Management told CreditSights buyers have shown interest and some deals could close this year or next. CreditSights still rates the bonds Buy for high-risk investors but calls cash “precarious,” noting Vista has never sold malls/offices/land bank to outsiders before; VistaREIT transfers keep assets inside the Villar orbit. Wider Villar reset already sold PrimeWater; rival retailers are moving into former AllDay and AllHome sites. Opener/COO take: soft landlords open lease renegotiation and dark-box hunting. If a south mall is on the block, map who buys it — a REIT transfer vs a third-party sale changes your landlord personality and CAPEX appetite for food tenants.
