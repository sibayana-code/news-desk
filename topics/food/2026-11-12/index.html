New chef at Modern Shanghai, new directions at The Bistro Group

11 Nov 2026 · BusinessWorld, with Dispatch MNL

Late in October, The Bistro Group opened SIKLAB+ (Filipino) and Denny's (American, open 24/7) together in one freestanding building on Kalayaan Avenue near Quezon City Hall. It is the first time the company has put two concepts under one roof, and a move away from its usual mall format. The group told BusinessWorld that freestanding stores work as "billboard stores", serve guests within one to three kilometers who don't want to deal with mall traffic and parking, can stay open past mall hours, have room for functions, and make rider pickups faster. It sees "big white spaces" for freestanding sites, naming Timog, Morato, Scout and Kalayaan as areas with the right mix of homes and offices.

Operator read: this is a mall-native group following where the customer is going. Delivery and the 24-hour crowd work better from the curb. The trade-off is that you own the foot traffic yourself, with no mall anchor bringing people in, so the site has to sit in a dense residential-plus-office pocket. In Calamba and along the national highway, the same checklist applies: parking, a 1–3 km rooftop count, and a frontage riders can find. Pairing two of your own concepts in one building also spreads the rent.

Korean bakery Standard Bread debuts in the Philippines

11 Nov 2026 · Inside Retail Asia

Korean bakery chain Standard Bread has opened its first Philippine flagship at SM Megamall. The concept is built on fresh bread with spreads, and the local menu adds French toast, brunch plates, salads, pasta, rice meals and coffee, including Crème Brûlée French Toast and Bulgogi Doenjang Risotto. The store also has "Standard Bread Village", a retail corner selling merchandise of its bread characters.

Operator read: another Korean café-bakery is betting on the all-day bread-and-brunch slot, and it added rice meals for the Filipino market from day one. The character merchandise is the other margin play: things people buy and take home that don't spoil. Expect more Korean bakery brands to follow into the big SM and Ayala malls south of Manila. A local bakery's defense is freshness, price and pasalubong, not trying to copy the look.
