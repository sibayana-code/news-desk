JFC flips Highlands control for $88M

23–24 Sep 2026

Jollibee Foods Corp. agreed to sell an 11% stake in SF Vung Tau JSC — parent of Vietnam’s Highlands Coffee — to partner Viet Thai International for about VND2.3 trillion (~$88.5M). Stake math: JFC 60%→49%, VTI/founder David Thai 40%→51%. Implied equity value ~$800M, a premium to book. Payment stretches after close with a capped upward adjustment tied to share value in that window; JFC keeps board seats and minority protections. Use of proceeds: cut debt, reinvest in higher-growth platforms, support shareholder returns. Highlands still targets a Vietnam IPO by Q1 2027. As of Q2, Highlands was 1,062 of JFC’s 10,767 system doors; COL flagged Highlands Q2 EBITDA at VND440.4B (~$16.9M), +70% YoY. Operator read for Calamba: this is the same JFC that already cut 2026 CapEx to ₱13–15B and gross new stores to 1,000–1,100 in August. Overseas, they harvest a mature coffee platform and hand majority back to the local founder before a listing. Domestically, that is not a cue to spam new franchise forms — it is a cue that capital is choosy. If you pitch or compete near a JFC door this ber season, assume the mothership is defending SSSG and unit economics, not blanketing every barangay.

First frozen steamed saba ships to Australia

22 Sep 2026

DA Davao’s agribusiness team facilitated Golden Flavours Pastries and Gourmet Inc.’s first export of frozen whole steamed saba (Cardava) bananas to Australia — 7,000 kg packed Sep 22, sailing in October, tentative Sydney arrival Nov 3. Fruit is harvested at 90–100% maturity, then blast-frozen at −18°C for up to a year of shelf life. Link came through the Sabah International Expo 2026; Mindanao Development Authority notes Marilog upland growers, including IP communities, in the supply chain. GM Razel Ann Suy Tan: the point is a market that pays farmers, not only a new SKU abroad. Operator read: this is value-add cold chain, the same physics you price when you blast-chill packed meats for a commissary — freeze the labor into the product so the boat can wait. Raw Cardava to Jeddah (chayote/sweet potato/singkamas wave in late August) is one lane; steamed-frozen to Sydney is the higher-margin lane. For a Calamba opener the steal is process discipline and IP-grower sourcing stories buyers will pay for — not chasing another fresh-produce gamble into Habagat weeks.

PSA: rice near ₱50, cabbage +37% MoM

18 Sep 2026

PSA’s first-phase September retail situationer (Sep 1–5, released Sep 18): six of seven monitored agri commodities rose vs early August. Regular milled rice averaged ₱49.96/kg — only +₱0.32 from August’s first phase, but +24% vs ₱40.31 a year earlier. Cabbage jumped to ₱125.86 from ₱91.63 (+37.3% MoM). Carabao mango ₱182.96 (+17.1%). Galunggong ₱248.53. Native garlic ₱173.36. Chicken egg ₱9.26/pc. Brown sugar eased to ₱71.55. National averages from selected outlets; provincial capitals only outside NCR. Operator read: rice is the sticky floor under every plate and every QSR ₱99 war — Jollibee’s Pepper Cream Fillet ₱99 window closed Sep 24 while McD still sells 1-pc McDo + rice + drink from ₱99 (FLAG: promo). Cabbage’s month-on-month whip is the kitchen risk this week: specials that lean on highland/lowland veg need alternate SKUs or a price that moves with the wet market. For Calamba, lock rice terms, float veg, and don’t let a finished JFC teaser trick you into matching a dead promo without knowing your food cost.
