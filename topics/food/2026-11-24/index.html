DA: holiday pork, chicken and fish should hold; El Niño puts vegetables at risk

23 Nov 2026 · Daily Tribune

Agriculture Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel Jr. said Monday there is enough pork and chicken to prevent big price jumps over Christmas, and that local tilapia and milkfish production keeps fish manageable even with the closed fishing season pushing more imports (imported galunggong ₱260–300/kg). Vegetables are the exception: he said prices "might fluctuate slightly because of El Niño," and DA monitoring already shows tomatoes up to ₱170/kg and carrots up to ₱180. On rice, he said ₱65/kg imported premium reflects specialty varieties, and regular local rice still sells at ₱38–52.

Operator read: this is the window to fix proteins. A minister on record saying "stable" is about as good as forward guidance gets in this market, so negotiate fixed December prices with your pork and chicken suppliers this week. Vegetables are where Q1 margin leaks, after El Niño already cut farm output. Cost your plates at today's ₱170 tomato, not October's, and line up substitutes (cucumber, sayote, pechay) your cooks can switch to without a staff memo.

Burned out in July, Aidalicious Chicken Inasal reopens in Santa Rosa after months on a food truck

23 Nov 2026 · PEP.ph

Say Alonzo and husband Ryan Tordesillas lost their Aidalicious Chicken Inasal by Aida's Bacolod restaurant to a fire on 25 July. They first grilled from home, which Alonzo said mostly lost money and drew a neighbor's complaint, then moved to a food truck at Vista Mall Santa Rosa beside Jollibee. There, pickleball players and Grab orders keep 13 staff on payroll. The restaurant reopens before December at Paseo Outlet, Santa Rosa, opposite Kanin Club and next to Café Breton. Alonzo said the original capital was her husband's retirement money and that the insurance payout was not enough, because they still owed the mall back rent.

Operator read: this is your category in your town. Three things to take from it. First, a truck at a mall with an anchor nearby (pickleball, Jollibee) plus delivery apps kept the brand alive and the staff paid until a new site was ready. That is a cheap bridge format worth keeping on file. Second, insurance that pays for the fire but not the rent you still owe is a gap many operators don't know they have. Third, a celebrity-linked inasal opening at Paseo, close to Nuvali, means more competition for the chicken plate in Santa Rosa in Q1. Expect promo noise at launch.
