Shakey's Pizza posts Q3 2026 earnings rebound as reset strategy pays off

12 Nov 2026 · Context.ph

Shakey's Pizza Asia Ventures (SPAVI) says core net income rose 11% year on year in Q3 and 51% from Q2, excluding one-time restructuring costs, which came mostly from scaling back its Peri-Peri Charcoal Chicken network. Systemwide sales reached ₱6.8B (up 11%), and same-store sales grew 3.5% after a flat first half. Management credits value promotions and price increases kept below inflation. Gross margin improved 100 basis points from Q2 to 21.2%, which it attributes to supply chain savings. The group has 3,106 stores and outlets, with 136 net new this year. Including the restructuring charges, headline net income was only ₱75M for the quarter. CEO Vicente Gregorio calls 2026 a "reset year".

Operator read: this is one public company's numbers showing that holding prices and running promos brought customers back. Margin came back through purchasing and supply chain savings, not through menu prices. Its weak spot was a secondary brand that grew too many stores and had to be cut back. If you run a second concept, put it through the same test: does each store pay for itself? With rice and pork prices unsettled into the holidays, the lesson is to hold the line on price and win the savings back in your supply chain.

DA says pork prices down despite farmers' complaints

12 Nov 2026 · Daily Tribune

Malacañang, citing DA monitoring, says retail pork prices are down from February. Local kasim went from ₱350 to ₱320/kg and local belly from ₱385 to ₱370/kg, while imported belly stayed at ₱300. Farm group SINAG counters that wet-market pork is still overpriced when farmgate prices are only ₱180–200/kg. The Federation of Free Farmers welcomed the government's 60-day suspension of some pork imports but says it came too late: imports and frozen stocks had already pushed farmgate prices below what it costs hog raisers to produce.

Operator read: the gap between what farms get and what markets charge is your opening. Hog raisers are losing money and want buyers. A restaurant group in Laguna or Batangas can sign directly with a raiser or a cooperative and an accredited slaughterhouse for steady weekly volume at a price tied to farmgate. The import pause could tighten frozen supply in December, so stock up on imported belly cuts now if your menu depends on them.
