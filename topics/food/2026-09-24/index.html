DA: August palay farmgate ₱22.16 (+29.9%); Calabarzon +50%

21 Sep 2026

Philstar / DA / PSA: August farmgate palay averaged ₱22.16 per kilo, up 29.9% from ₱17.06 a year earlier. Agriculture Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel Jr. said the jump gives farmers room to absorb fuel, transport and input costs and should help planting decisions as a “severe” El Niño is expected later this year. Regional lift: Ilocos +54%, Calabarzon +50%, Cagayan Valley +41%, Central Luzon +32%; Central Visayas led levels at ₱28.07 (+41%). Monsoon-hit pockets stayed soft where wet grain cut quality. DA is lining interventions for productivity, weather disruption support, and rice supply/price stability. Operator read for Calamba: this is not a cheap-rice story. Yesterday’s tape already had retail regular milled near ₱50 and Q3 standing-crop cuts. Higher farmgate with high retail means the middle of the chain is squeezed — lock miller and trade quotes now, watch Calabarzon wet-market grades, and treat El Niño talk as a Q4–Q1 plate-cost scenario, not a Manila headline.

SaladStop: ≥60% plant protein, tied to 2030 net-zero

29 Aug 2026

Insider PH: SaladStop! Group set a global target that at least 60% of its protein sourcing come from plant-based ingredients, nested inside a 2030 net-zero operations goal. The Singapore-based group runs more than 80 outlets across Singapore, the Philippines, Indonesia, South Korea, Thailand, Hong Kong and other markets, serving over five million people a year under SaladStop!, Heybo, Wooshi, Smoosha and FreshKitchen. Plant proteins already show up as legumes, seitan, nuts, seeds, whole grains, leafy greens and mushrooms. CEO Adrien Desbaillets framed it as variety and balance, not sacrifice; Lever Foundation’s Marielle Lagulay called the 60% mark a clear benchmark other foodservice operators can copy. The group was first in Asia to carbon-label its full menu (2020) and took B Corp in 2023. Operator read: this is the Insider PH model — brand strategy with a number you can put on a sourcing sheet. For a Calamba opener, steal the structure (one KPI, one date, menu-visible) whether you chase plant share, Laguna farm kg, or rice-plate carbon. Don’t copy the salad format; copy the measurable sourcing bet.

24 Sep 2026

Jollibee: The two-week ₱99 Pepper Cream Chicken Fillet window closes today (Sep 24) at participating stores nationwide for dine-in, take-out and drive-thru while supplies last.
