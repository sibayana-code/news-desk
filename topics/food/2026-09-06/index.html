RestoPH: stack the discount, kill the promo

6 Sep 2026

Restaurant Owners of the Philippines — Mama Lou’s co-founder David Sison, with leaders from Jollibee, McDonald’s, Pizza Hut, and KFC — met to align against pending House bills that reshape who pays for senior and PWD discounts. Focus: House Bill 16 (Rep. Martin Romualdez), which would let the statutory 20% apply on top of promotional prices. Today seniors/PWDs pick promo or 20%, whichever is higher — not both. Sison: promos are volume plays on razor-thin margins; load mandated cost onto them and “the math stops working,” so promos disappear and effective prices rise for the guests who needed them. History: 1992 benefit was a recoverable tax credit; 2004 switched to a tax deduction and dropped the income ceiling — of every P20 discount, operators recover ~P5. Supermarkets got design (5%, basic necessities, weekly caps); restaurants got 20% on everything including wagyu and wine. RestoPH ask: expand the benefit if Congress wants, but restore tax-credit funding in the same bill. Group has ~230 Metro Manila members; 49% are small (1–5 branches). Also flagging LPG, rent, and utilities freezing hiring and cutting hours. Operator read: this is your P&L before it is politics. Run a week of tickets with stacked 20% on every promo SKU — if the contribution margin flips negative, you either kill the promo or raise the rack. Watch the bill text, not the press conference.

DA: P20/kg market rice is out of reach

5 Sep 2026

Agriculture Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel Jr. told One News’ Storycon the campaign P20-per-kilo market price for rice cannot be hit without subsidy — fertilizer, transport, and fuel keep production costs too high. Urea was ~P700/sack when the promise was made; now ~P2,600 (had hit ~P3,000/50kg before Marcos took office; Middle East pressure again). DA market watch Aug 24–30: locally produced regular-milled P44.94/kg, well-milled P47.73, premium P52.76. Policy pivot is already live: “Benteng Bigas, Meron Na!” through ~800 Kadiwa outlets, 12M+ beneficiaries as of July, 10–20k MT of P20 rice monthly targeting 40k MT by yearend, funded partly with P9B NFA procurement — program scheduled through June 30, 2028. Tiu Laurel: no rice supply shortage now; ~67k of 75k monsoon-hit hectares were rice; NFA stocks frontloaded; imports after harvest to buffer severe El Niño (−700k MT local output expected). Kitchen read: yesterday’s 19.4% rice inflation number and today’s admission are the same story. Lock commercial rice through Q1; treat P20 as a Kadiwa channel, not a wholesale quote.

Bulacan airport: European banks, not a rumor

5 Sep 2026

San Miguel’s Ramon Ang: New Manila International Airport (NMIA) in Bulacan — ~P740B — is being financed by European banks, not domestic borrowing, so the build must clear international lender standards. Danish Hydraulic Institute designed drainage around rivers, coast, storms, sea-level rise, and land subsidence; Mott MacDonald (UK) reviewed/combined the models; Boskalis (Dutch) did land development and still draws technical-supervision fees. Dutch export-credit agency Atradius insures financing tied to the Boskalis contract. Foreign lender reps due on-site in coming weeks; Philippine banks also want into airport and aerotropolis financing. Project previously briefed at OECD as an emerging-market E&S case. Opener read: this is a north-corridor catchment story. When European money and Dutch/UK engineering stay on the job, lodging, QSR, and wholesale demand along the Bulacan–NLEx spine gets a firmer timeline than flood-headline noise. Calamba/south operators: treat it as competitive gravity north — not your next lease, but a demand shift to price into 2027–28 site picks.
