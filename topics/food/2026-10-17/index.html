Takes

  • Don’t read ₱49.79 as the rice problem fading. The fertilizer shock is a lagged cost: PIDS research has rice up ₱5.0–₱10.6/kg over two to three years from the fertilizer channel alone, and IFAD is now saying the harvest effect is showing up. If your rice supplier will hold price for 60–90 days at today’s level, take it this month. Open
  • Unlimited rice is the line that breaks first. If you run it, or a rice-heavy value meal, price it on a ₱55–₱60/kg landed assumption rather than today’s ₱50 and see if it still clears margin. If it doesn’t, change the rules now (one refill, or unlimited only on dine-in combos) while it still looks like a menu refresh and not a hike.

IFAD: the Hormuz shock is reaching harvests, and the Philippines buys 90% of its fertilizer

15 Oct 2026

Reuters, via Arab News: International Fund for Agricultural Development president Alvaro Lario said Thursday that the impact of the Strait of Hormuz shipping disruption is “now starting to be seen” in agricultural harvests. Fertilizer and fuel stocks cushioned the first months. Now prolonged uncertainty means more cautious planting, less fertilizer applied, and higher fuel costs for mechanized harvesting. He added El Niño and Black Sea wheat disruptions to the cost stack, and warned that small farmers may shift from higher-value, slower crops to faster-cash ones. A separate official told Reuters that if the US and Iran fail to agree within weeks on a UN-backed plan for moving fertilizer through Hormuz, parts of Asia and Africa could face a food security crisis.

The local numbers line up. PIDS research says the Philippines imports about 90% of its fertilizer and modeled regular-milled rice rising 9.1% to 19.4%, roughly ₱5.0 to ₱10.6/kg, from the fertilizer channel alone, with full adjustment over two to three years. UNDP put domestic urea up about 37% after the shock, from around ₱1,650 to ₱2,255 per 50-kg bag. Meanwhile PSA’s first October reading has rice at ₱49.79/kg, slightly below late September, and DA’s September data has rice inflation at 20.3%.

Operator read: the shelf price is flat-ish while the farm cost is still climbing. That gap usually closes against the buyer. Three moves: (1) ask your rice and corn-fed protein suppliers for a held price through year-end and take a modest premium for it; (2) re-cost any unlimited-rice or rice-heavy value meal at a higher landed price before the Christmas menu locks; (3) watch the shift to faster-cash crops, because it can thin out the slower vegetables and fruit you take for granted. Flag: foreign wire story plus research projections, not a DA price announcement.

The Lunch Lady opens Oct 29 in BGC: noodle soup at lunch, charcoal and cocktails at dinner

15 Oct 2026

Insider PH: The Lunch Lady, which started in 1995 as Nguyễn Thị Thành’s rotating noodle-soup stall in Ho Chi Minh City and blew up after Anthony Bourdain featured it in 2009, opens its Manila restaurant on Oct 29 at 102 Forbestown Road, BGC. It’s the group’s return to Southeast Asia after Vancouver and Toronto, where it has collected Michelin Bib Gourmands. The local partner is HTCG Premium Food Concepts, led by Annabelle Chua. The menu runs two programs: traditional Vietnamese noodle soups at lunch and contemporary Southeast Asian cooking at dinner, with Wagyu phở, steak lúc lắc and garlic noodles, charcoal cooking, local sourcing and craft cocktails. The same week, Reese Fernandez’s Tara Filipino Diner opened its own BGC branch.

Operator read: BGC keeps pulling in foreign brands with a story and a local operator partner, and Forbestown is getting crowded. For an opener outside the capital, the lesson is the format, not the zip code. One kitchen running a fast, cheap lunch and a slower, higher-check dinner gets more out of rent than an all-day menu that’s mediocre at both. Watch the first month’s lunch price point and queue. If phở at lunch lands near QSR value-meal money while dinner carries the margin, that’s a template that travels to a Nuvali or Calamba office-plus-family catchment. Flag: launch announcement, so no pricing or traffic data yet.
