Takes

  • ₱89 is the new floor for a branded rice bowl, and it comes from inside the Jollibee group, under Jollibee’s own ₱99. If your value line sits at ₱110–₱130 with no clear protein or topping story, you’re now priced against a drive-thru. Don’t match it. Build one bowl at ₱99–₱109 that looks bigger (egg, crunchy topping, visible sauce) and earn the margin on the drink. Open
  • Coffee is splitting in two: Compose at ₱90 for a 20-oz Americano, Bacha at ₱399 with theatre and gift boxes. A ₱150–₱220 café cup with neither price nor show is the one in trouble. If coffee is on your menu, pick a side, and with Christmas coming, a small shelf of beans or gift packs is cheap to test.
  • The rain damage is small next to El Niño, and the dry-spell peak is now January. That means the vegetable and rice squeeze lands in Q1, right after the holiday rush. Lock Q1 supply prices in November, and write the January menu so it leans less on leafy highland vegetables.

Mang Inasal goes to ₱89 with Sulit Bowls, ten pesos under Jollibee’s own value line

17 Oct 2026

Wazzup.PH: Mang Inasal launched Sulit Bowls from ₱89 in Metro Manila and Luzon, with Visayas and Mindanao due within the month. There are two variants. Soy Garlic Chicken is grilled chicken with garlic bits, spring onion, sliced egg and soy-garlic sauce over rice, and Pork Sisig is sisig with creamy sauce over rice. They sell through dine-in, takeout, drive-thru and delivery. President Mike V. Castro pitched it as delicious, filling, convenient and affordable. It lands while sister brand Jollibee runs Burger Steak bundles from ₱99 through Dec 31.

Operator read: both bowls are built on cost-friendly protein. Sisig runs on pork, and PSA had fresh pork with bones at ₱295.58/kg in late September, down from ₱327.03 a year earlier. Chicken in a bowl stretches less meat across more rice. A single-vessel bowl is also faster on the line and travels better on delivery than a plated meal. For an independent the copy-paste move is format, not price: one or two rice bowls built on a cheaper cut, an egg and a signature sauce, priced just above ₱100, with a drink attach. Watch whether JFC’s brands end up competing with each other on price, because a ₱89–₱99 band held through Christmas resets what guests expect a quick lunch to cost. Flag: brand release, no volume or margin data.

Bacha Coffee bets ₱399 a cup on Manila’s 8 pm coffee queues

17 Oct 2026

ABS-CBN: Bacha Coffee, founded in Marrakech in 1910 and now in 16 countries with more than 50 branches, opened at SM Mall of Asia and Greenbelt 3 this month. Owner and chief commercial officer Maranda Barnes said the Philippines stood out for its coffee culture, with people queuing for coffee even at 8 pm. A cup is ₱399 and comes with vanilla Chantilly cream. The stores retail 200-plus 100% arabica varieties from 35 countries, ground to order and packed in canisters or gift boxes, and serve croissants, breakfast and lunch. Barnes pointed Filipino diners to a soy-marinated chicken croissant she likened to adobo. The goal she named is becoming a household name.

Operator read: this is coffee sold as a gift and an occasion, not a caffeine hit. Two months after Jollibee brought Compose Coffee in at ₱90, the market now has both ends served by well-funded brands. The playbook worth stealing isn’t the price. It’s the retail wall: beans, canisters and gift boxes turn a café into a Christmas shop with no extra seats. If you run coffee in a Calamba or Nuvali site, a small seasonal gifting shelf is a low-risk way to lift the ticket in November and December. Flag: brand interview at launch, no sales data.
