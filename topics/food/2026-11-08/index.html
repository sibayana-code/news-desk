Recovery Food shuts down after 14 years in BGC

5 Nov 2026 · Radar

Filipino comfort-food restaurant Recovery Food has closed its remaining branch at BGC Crossroads in Taguig. Passersby said it had been shut since 1 November, with a storefront sign thanking customers for “14 years of Hunger, Hangover and Heartbreak.” The brand did not announce the closure online; its last social post was a Halloween promo on 31 October. Opened in 2012, Recovery Food sold Filipino rice meals, and its Crossroads and Stopover branches ran 24/7 for BGC’s graveyard-shift workers and late-night crowd. Its Alabang, Pasay and Quezon City branches had already closed. Radar says the reason is unclear and lists it among a run of recent business closures.

Operator read: a 24/7 rice-meal concept lives on night labor and night power, and both got dearer while BPO and nightlife traffic shifted. Before you add late hours, cost the graveyard shift line by line and check what share of sales really lands after midnight. The bigger lesson is the slow shrink: three branches closed before the flagship did, which usually means the brand stopped being the default and became a nostalgia pick. Watch for the BGC lease. A well-known corner with a cleared kitchen is a cheaper fit-out than raw space if you are hunting for Metro Manila exposure.
