Two brands, one landmark destination: Mango Tree and Wong Place open at Alabang West

3 Nov 2026 · Promo (launch write-up, Where's RR)

A new two-restaurant destination has opened at Alabang West on Daang Hari Road, Las Piñas. Mango Tree, the Thai chain that started in Bangkok in 1994 and now has over 45 locations across seven markets, opened its fourth Philippine flagship there, with high ceilings, seating for 200 and private rooms for celebrations. It sits beside Wong Place, a homegrown modern Chinese concept started in 2023, now on its third branch, serving dim sum and a cocktail list for late nights. The pitch is long lunches, milestones and nights out that run past dinner. Mango Tree in the Philippines runs over 13 stores across three concepts and says more are coming nationwide.

Operator read: this is a launch write-up, so skip the adjectives and look at the setup. Pairing a celebration restaurant with a late-night bar-style sister on one lot lets a guest start at one and finish at the other, keeping the whole evening's spend in the group. Private rooms target the birthday, reunion and company-dinner trade that South families drive for. If you're scouting south of Alabang, note that Daang Hari is filling with destination dining, so a Laguna site has to win on being closer, not on novelty. Check their price per head and how full the private rooms are on a weekend before you size one of your own.
