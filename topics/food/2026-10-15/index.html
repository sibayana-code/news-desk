KFC’s ₱90 combo undercuts Jollibee’s new ₱99 Burger Steak meal

14 Oct 2026

Bilyonaryo: Jollibee has lowered the price of its one-piece Burger Steak meal with fries to ₱99 after customers complained that comparable meals at rival chains cost less. KFC, the piece notes, is offering a ₱90 combo that includes a main dish, a side, and a soft drink. The comparison lands amid growing scrutiny of Jollibee’s menu prices and whether they still justify the premium. Operator read: this is the overnight frame change from yesterday’s ASTIG promo write-up. The ₱99 Saucy-Sarap Savers line (through Dec 31) was the holiday floor; KFC’s ₱90 full combo just stepped under it. For a Calamba or Metro Manila opener, two practical moves. First, walk both counters this week and photograph the actual tray — what is on the board vs what lands on the tray — before you rewrite your own lunch specials. Second, decide deliberately: match the ₱90–99 band on one everyday rice meal through year-end, or sit clearly above it and sell taste the way some guests already defend Jollibee’s longganisa over McDo’s cheaper breakfast. Flag: brand promo / price fight coverage, not a cost-of-goods story.

McDo bets on Taste of Korea and app bundles, not a cheaper list price

12 Oct 2026

Insider PH: McDonald’s Philippines expanded Taste of Korea on Oct 9 with its largest Korean-inspired lineup yet — Yangnyeom McSpicy, Yangnyeom Double Cheeseburger, Galbi McShaker Fries, and K-Melon Float — after September’s McSpicy collaboration with K-pop artist G-DRAGON. Brand manager Karl Christian Chua said Korean food and culture continue to unite Pinoys across ages. Beyond the kitchen, the chain is running a microdrama, “The Plus One,” about three friends competing for a Korea trip, and pushing McDo App deals: Yangnyeom McSpicy with a free large Sprite for ₱199, and Yangnyeom Double Cheeseburger with a free large Sprite for ₱169. Available dine-in, takeout, drive-thru, and delivery across close to 800 restaurants. Operator read: while Jollibee and KFC slug it out under ₱100, McDo is testing whether culture LTOs and app-locked free drinks can hold check averages in the mid-to-high ₱100s. That is the playbook if your protein cost will not let you print a ₱99 rice meal — rotate a limited flavor, put the deal in the app, keep the board price intact. The network size is the point: ~800 doors is how you learn fast whether a cultural LTO outlasts the promo window. Pair this with the BPI ₱1.75 add-on that dies Oct 19 if you are still riding bank-linked traffic.
