Max's profit doubles as diners come back, Figaro cuts costs to grow 69%, and Mang Inasal takes its ₱89 bowls nationwide

Tape

  • NCR prices · DA monitoring for 18 Nov: local well-milled rice ₱49.09/kg (flat), regular-milled ₱44.87 (down 2.8%), red onion ₱129.87 (up 3.0%), local garlic ₱385 (up 1.7%), carrots ₱146.04 (down 8.5%), tomato ₱123.45 (down 5.9%), medium egg ₱10.20 (down 3.7%). (eAgri, DA data)
  • Figaro Culinary · Q3 profit up 69% to ₱170.9M on 6.8% revenue growth and 5.3% lower opex. Gross margin slipped to 46.1% from 46.6% on raw material costs. Parent is tendering ₱0.82/share to delist from the PSE. (BusinessWorld, 19 Nov)
  • Openings · Texas Roadhouse (The Bistro Group) opened its 26th PH store at Robinsons Antipolo (Manila Standard, 19 Nov). Sukiya opened its first Central Luzon branch at SM City Marilao (Dot Daily Dose, 19 Nov · Flag: promo-style mall post).

Takes

  • Max's and Figaro both grew profit by cutting, not by price: store closures, lower rent and maintenance, sharper purchasing and commissary at Max's; less marketing and payroll at Figaro. Before December, go through your weakest stores and your commissary buying the same way.
  • Both chains saw cost of goods rise faster than sales. Figaro's margin lost half a point to raw materials. Pork is cheaper now, but onion and garlic keep climbing in NCR. Check sauce and marinade costs first.
  • Mang Inasal's ₱89 rice bowl is now the national price floor for a chicken or sisig meal. If your cheapest single meal is well above that, have a clear reason to pay more, or add a value bowl of your own.

Max's Q3 profit more than doubles as same-store sales come back

19 Nov 2026 · BusinessWorld

Max's Group made ₱25.0M attributable profit in July–September, up from ₱10.0M. Same-store sales rose 6.8% after shrinking 2.7% a year earlier. Transactions were up 4.7% and average spend per transaction up 3%. Revenue rose 8.3% to ₱2.95B. Utilities, fuel, supplies and payroll pushed costs up. Delivery-platform and agency charges drove a 9.7% rise in G&A, and ad spend nearly doubled to ₱73M. Store closures, lower rent and maintenance, and better purchasing and commissary work offset part of that. The network shrank to 554 stores from 566.

Operator read: the recovery came from more visits, not higher prices. Spend per transaction rose only 3%. Max's paid for that with heavier advertising and kept margin by closing weak stores. Delivery-platform fees are now named as a G&A drag at a listed chain, so recheck what share of your sales go through the apps and what they really net after commission.

Mang Inasal takes ₱89 SulitBowls to Visayas and Mindanao

19 Nov 2026 · The Manila Times (brand release)

After launching in Luzon and Metro Manila, SulitBowls are now sold nationwide from ₱89, in Soy Garlic Chicken and Pork Sisig, for dine-in, takeout, drive-through and delivery. The chain also launched a limited Tsoko n' Nut Yema Halo-Halo, available until 31 Dec. Flag: this is a company release on the newswire.

Operator read: a Jollibee-group brand is putting a single rice-bowl price into every region before Christmas. That's the "savings" side of the QSR fight. The bowl format serves one protein on rice, which keeps portions tight and holds up in delivery. The holiday halo-halo is there to raise the ticket. If you run a casual or Filipino concept, copy the pairing: an entry price that brings people in and a seasonal dessert that raises the bill.
