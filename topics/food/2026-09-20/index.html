Nestlé doubles down on PH — Tanauan ₱500M Chuckie line, Group CEO names top growth market

10 + 18 Sep 2026

BusinessWorld: Nestlé Philippines inaugurated a half-billion-peso expansion of its Chuckie chocolate-drink factory in Tanauan City, Batangas (First Philippine Industrial Park) on Sep 10 — a new filling line sized for about 144 million additional packs a year. Chairman/CEO Mauricio Alarcón framed the CapEx as confidence in local manufacturing and Chuckie demand; beverages BEO Veronica Carmen Cruz said tough times reinforce the case for affordable nutrition made here, less exposed to global supply shocks. Tanauan Mayor Sonny Collantes stressed jobs and local supplier spillover. Separately on Sep 18, Nestlé Group CEO Philipp Navratil, after a PH visit, called the country one of Nestlé’s most dynamic growth markets — young demographics, brand connection, room to invest. Nestlé PH booked $3.2B revenue in 2025 (largest SEA market, sixth globally). Local plants: Cabuyao and Canlubang in Laguna; Lipa and Tanauan in Batangas; plus Pulilan and Cagayan de Oro. Operator read for Calamba: this is the South Luzon industrial corridor vote — same freight lanes, same labor pool, same weekend mall gravity as Nuvali–Tagaytay. When a global F&B player keeps filling lines in Tanauan and Cabuyao, it is a signal on demand durability, not a press release about Milo. Watch wage and logistics pressure on that belt as you price commissary and staff meals.

Hikiniku to Come hits SM Megamall #2 — rice-burger format under Standard Group

17 Sep 2026

Inside Retail Asia: Japan’s Hikiniku to Come opened its second Philippine branch at SM Megamall, with a third location already targeted for 2027 and more in the pipeline. The Tokyo 2020 brand’s signature is “on-the-rice” burger meals — patties grilled only after order, served over fresh rice with condiments, not a bun-first QSR plate. Local operator is The Standard Group (est. 2011), which already runs Yabu, Ippudo, Elephant Grounds, and Kiwami across 37 locations. COO/opener take: this is a format bet, not a price fight. Made-to-order rice plates at Mega rent prove mid-premium Japanese still clears Orthus foot traffic — useful as you size Inasal Bites / commissary positioning against mall food courts. The tell for Laguna is whether Standard Group’s next doors stay Mega/Ortigas-class or probe south (Nuvali corridor, Tagaytay weekend). Until a south pin drops, treat this as Metro format intel, not an immediate Calamba competitor — but the rice-as-base plate is the product lesson.

McD Asia Chicken + Friday fries — K-flavor LTO meets habit promo (FLAG)

7 + 17–18 Sep 2026

2nd Opinion / Insider PH: McDonald’s Philippines launched its Asia Chicken Campaign with G-DRAGON from Sep 17 — a 13-market Korea-led push introducing McSpicy (whole-cut thigh sandwich, spicy crunch) and McNuggets with Cajun Sauce, plus PEACEMINUSONE merch and digital activations. Separately, Insider PH’s Sep 7 ops read on unlimited fries Fridays (live since Jul 10) says ≥350,000 customers join each Friday; the chain cooks close to six-digit kilograms of fries daily and locks supply plans in the prior year’s Q4 so campaigns do not stock-out. Marketing director Yves Nakpil: success is participation and habit, not just ticket count; chicken stays #1 SKU, fries ride as add-on. FLAG: both are promo/LTO. Operator take vs Jollibee’s ₱99 Pepper Cream Fillet combos (Sep 9–24): McD is selling culture + ritual (K-chicken, Friday fries hour); Jollibee is selling sub-₱100 value. Do not chase both. For a Calamba opener, the week’s QSR gravity will siphon trial meals — keep your plate math honest, and if you promo at all, pick one lane (value or signature) so you are not fighting two national media budgets with a local flyer.
