Jollibee Group Philippines identifies over 1,500 potential trade areas

29 Oct 2026

Jollibee Foods Corp. says its network assessment found more than 1,500 potential trade areas for 2027–2030 across Jollibee, Mang Inasal and Chowking, with about 71% outside Metro Manila. The Philippine business had more than 3,500 stores as of the first half of 2026. Systemwide sales grew 10% in both 2024 and 2025, with same-store sales up 8% and 5%. Jollibee alone is about 62% of Philippine systemwide sales, and the three brands together are nearly 90%. Growth will mix new stores with delivery, drive-thru, digital ordering and flexible formats. Mang Inasal’s first drive-thru opened in Sta. Maria, Bulacan in December 2025, and by H1 2026 its eight drive-thrus averaged daily sales about 40% above its other stores. JFC says actual openings still depend on site availability and viability.

Operator read: same-store growth fell from 8% to 5%, so new stores are now doing more of the work. That means more JFC site hunters in provincial towns, competing for the same corner lots, highway frontage and mall pads you want. The drive-thru number is the useful part. A 40% sales lift from a car lane tells you the south Luzon highway corridors (SLEX, CALAX, the Calamba–Sto. Tomas stretch) reward stores built for cars. If you’re scouting a site, rate drive-thru potential first, and look for a lot with room for a car lane before JFC does.

DA sees imported rice moving up by November; corn import quota raised to 500,000 MT

30 Oct 2026

DA Assistant Secretary Arnel de Mesa told reporters on Thursday that the peso’s slide will make imported rice and fertilizer more expensive, with “possible movements” in imported rice prices by November as freight also rises on fuel. On Oct 28, DA’s daily index had imported premium rice at ₱56.09/kg and imported well-milled at ₱48.58/kg. Rice imports reached 4.36 million MT as of Oct 22, with Vietnam supplying 2.78 million MT. DA says prices will not reach ₱60/kg. To cut feed costs, the government issued EO 129, which raises the corn import quota to 500,000 MT from 220,000 MT. DA says this should help keep livestock and poultry prices stable.

Operator read: the rice call repeats yesterday’s, so what’s new is the corn quota. It shows the government is working on meat costs through feed, not by cutting import tariffs. That’s good for chicken and pork costs in the first half of 2027, but the El Niño drought still runs to mid-2027. Plan for flat to slightly higher protein costs through the holidays. Use the quota to push back if a supplier asks for a feed-driven price hike in January.
