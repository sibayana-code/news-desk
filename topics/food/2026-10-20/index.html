Mang Inasal keeps growing in double digits, and Jollibee is courting more franchisees

19 Oct 2026

Insider PH: Mang Inasal, which runs more than 600 stores nationwide, posted 13.2% systemwide sales growth in the first half of 2026, on top of 15.6% for the whole of 2025. Takeout, delivery and drive-thru grew at a double-digit pace, giving the chain more ways to reach customers outside its restaurants. Mang Inasal president Mike Castro credited the Chicken Inasal and pork barbecue. The company says it’s refining its marination and grilling so quality holds as the network grows. Brand Finance ranked it the Philippines’ strongest quick-service brand in 2026, at 95.2 out of 100 (AAA+). Parent Jollibee Foods Corp. said franchise interest keeps rising, and Jollibee Group Philippines CEO Joseph Tanbuntiong said the group’s supply chain and franchise development team will back the expansion.

Operator read: this is a recruitment pitch as much as a results story. Jollibee wants more franchisee money to fund Mang Inasal’s rollout, which means more grilled-chicken outlets landing in provincial malls and town centers, including the new Laguna and Batangas strips. If you run a chicken or rice-meal concept, assume a Mang Inasal opens near you within two years and compete where it’s weakest: dine-in comfort, local flavors, and the groups and occasions a ₱99 value set doesn’t serve. If you’re thinking of buying a franchise instead, ask for unit-level numbers by store age and the split between dine-in and off-premise, not the systemwide growth figure. Flag: company statement, no store-level or franchise economics disclosed.

Super Typhoon Champi nears PAR, where it will be named Rosal

20 Oct 2026

PAGASA’s 4 am bulletin put Super Typhoon Champi 1,605 km east of Central Luzon, with maximum winds of 195 km/h and gusts up to 240 km/h, moving west-northwest at 20 km/h. Its trough is already bringing scattered rain and thunderstorms to Catanduanes and Northern and Eastern Samar, and seas off eastern Luzon and the Visayas are moderate to rough. PAGASA’s threat outlook expects it to enter the northeastern part of PAR this week. OneNews.PH quoted PAGASA weather specialist Grace Castañeda saying its winds may be felt by Wednesday, Oct 21, with rain intensifying in the north later in the week. Metro Manila and the rest of Luzon only get isolated thunderstorms for now.

Operator read: the track isn’t fixed, and PAGASA lists recurving away from land as one common October path. But if it hits northern Luzon, Benguet highland vegetables are what move first, right as pork and vegetable prices had been easing. Don’t panic-buy. Do check this week how many days of cold storage you have, ask your vegetable supplier what they’re holding, and keep a sub list ready for cabbage, carrots and potatoes in case the price spikes.
