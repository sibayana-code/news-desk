Shopee launches ShopeeFood in the Philippines, expanding its ecosystem of everyday services

13 Nov 2026 · ABS-CBN Lifestyle

Shopee has launched ShopeeFood in Metro Manila, making the Philippines its fifth Southeast Asian market for food delivery. Restaurants on the app range from quick-service chains and local favourites to cafes, bakeries and snack shops, and Shopee says it will bring in more national chains. It is folding food into its usual sale calendar (Weekday Flash Deals, Payday Sales, Double Day Mega Sales). New users get up to 75% off their first order, free delivery and a 50%-off voucher.

Operator read: Shopee already has the customers and the payments, so it will spend to buy orders in the first few months. That makes this a good time to get in early on good terms: low or waived commission, the platform paying for vouchers, and placement in the Payday and Double Day campaigns. Keep the menu on Shopee lean and priced the same as on Grab and foodpanda, so you don't train customers to wait for the cheaper app. It only covers Metro Manila for now. Laguna stores should watch for the rollout, not plan around it.

This matcha cafe from Malaysia is finally in the Philippines, bringing its signature six-gram blends

13 Nov 2026 · 8List.ph

Malaysian beverage brand Forest Cloud has opened its first Philippine store at the ground floor of Greenhills Mall, San Juan. It sells matcha lattes made with six grams of matcha per cup, plus flavoured versions, and is open during mall hours. Inquirer Lifestyle listed it as the lead new opening for November, noting how many Japanese, Chinese, Korean and homegrown matcha concepts have opened in recent months.

Operator read: foreign drink brands keep picking Metro Manila malls for their first store, so Laguna and Batangas malls are still open for a matcha or specialty drinks concept with a clear point of difference. Watch the cost: six grams of good matcha per cup is an expensive drink to make. If you copy the idea, work out your cost per cup before you set the price, and treat the high dose as the main selling point.
