Taiwan eyes PH restaurants and processed food, but says its pork won’t come in volume for three years

27 Oct 2026

Inquirer reports Taiwanese industry groups see room in Philippine restaurants and processed food, but not as a bulk pork supplier. Jennifer Chang of the Commerce Development Research Institute said Taiwanese pork will likely hold a small share because it is relatively expensive and limited for export, and that large frozen or chilled volumes are unlikely over the next three years. The group is brokering private supply deals and points to sausages, noodles and bubble tea as the stronger restaurant play. Bureau of Animal Industry data show Taiwan sent just 23,342 kg of pork in 2025, down 94.6% from 2024; DA lifted its ASF ban on Taiwanese pork in April.

Operator read: this closes one hope for the holiday squeeze. With DA stopping import permits from mid-November into January, there is no cheap new origin coming to cover liempo and kasim. Lock December–January pork prices now, local or imported. The opener angle is the other half of the story: Taiwanese groups want PH partners for sausages, noodles and tea. If you run a commissary, a Taiwanese sausage or braised-pork line is a co-pack conversation worth having.

Promo: McDo adds Creamy BBQ and Honey Butter to its ₱59/₱79 Snack and Match line

26 Oct 2026

A McDonald’s Philippines newswire piece in the Manila Times (promotional) launches three limited-time Snack and Match items: Honey Butter McShaker Fries, Creamy BBQ Burger McDo and Creamy BBQ Crispy Chicken Sandwich, at ₱59 solo or ₱79 as a combo. The promo art says it runs Oct 1 to Nov 11, 2026, across counter, drive-thru, McDelivery and the delivery apps.

Operator read: the build is the lesson. Same bun, same patty, same fries; only the sauce and seasoning change. That gives McDo a “new” reason to visit at the same food cost while pork and peso pressure build. If you have a merienda slot, a limited sauce rotation on an existing protein is the fastest test you can run before Christmas.
