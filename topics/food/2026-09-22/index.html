Ma Mon Luk Quezon Ave ordered closed — sanitation + tax notice; reopen eyed Sep 22

18–21 Sep 2026

radar / PEP: Quezon City Business Permits and Licensing Department posted a closure notice on Ma Mon Luk’s Quezon Avenue facade citing alleged violations of Presidential Decree 856 (Sanitation Code of the Philippines) and City Ordinance SP-91, S-93 (QC Revenue Code of 1993 as amended). Staff told radar they stopped operating around Sep 17; the brand’s Facebook said only “Temporarily Closed. We’ll be back soon.” Cleaning and equipment swaps (wooden siopao steamers → stainless) were underway with a hoped reopen around Sep 22. Ma Mon Luk International Corp. holds the 1997 permit; several Metro branches once existed — only Quezon Ave remains of a brand that started as street mami in the 1920s and a first restaurant in the 1950s. Operator read for Calamba: this is not celebrity nostalgia — it is LGU risk. Sanitation Code + local revenue paper can shut a heritage door while you still have regulars. Before holiday volume, walk wet floors, steamers, grease traps, and business-permit currency with the same seriousness you give plate cost. One remaining branch means one padlock ends the brand’s physical footprint.

McD × G-DRAGON Asia Chicken — McSpicy + Cajun Nuggets hit PH

21 Sep 2026

Corner Magazine PH: McDonald’s Philippines launched its Asia Chicken Campaign with G-DRAGON across 13 Asian markets (led by McDonald’s Korea), with the PH debut dated September 17. Local LTOs are McSpicy — whole-cut chicken thigh fillet in spicy crisp seasoning on a toasted bun with lettuce and mayo — and Chicken McNuggets with creamy Cajun Sauce, positioned as a K-inspired dip. Activations include digital content, limited experiences, and PEACEMINUSONE merchandise. Brand manager Karl Christian Chua framed it as ICON×ICON and Korean flavor culture. Operator take: this is a taste-and-culture chicken play, not another sub-₱100 value combo. Against Jollibee’s film-tied Forgotten Island bundle (nationwide from Sep 23 — flag as promo), McD is selling heat and K-drop while JFC sells friendship memory. If your plate is chicken, the week’s signal is pick a lane: spicy shareable sandwich energy vs value-meal nostalgia — don’t try to win both with one SKU.

SM Sto. Tomas +30% in Oct; Nuvali garden-dining flagship Nov 27

1 Sep 2026 (Q4 clock)

Rappler / GMA: SM Supermalls opens SM Nuvali on November 27 — about 82,000 sqm GLA, the group’s 91st Philippine mall and fifth (largest) in Laguna — with a one-hectare indoor garden using a two-layer membrane (Gardens by the Bay–style) so restaurants overlook greenery without outdoor heat. Steven Tan called out a break from “cookie-cutter” boxes toward local-adaptive architecture. Same Q4 provincial push: SM Sto. Tomas in Batangas expands roughly 30% GLA with a “Green Haven” (cinemas, coworking, wellness) targeted for October; SM Naga and SM Iloilo also expand; H1 mall occupancy hit a record 96% with revenues up 8% to ₱41.8B. Pipeline names Tagum, General Trias, Bohol, Malolos. Operator read for Calamba: Sto. Tomas is your Batangas catchment thickening now; Nuvali’s restaurant-ring garden is the Santa Rosa lifestyle magnet two months out. You already clocked Nuvali Chili’s / Matcha naming earlier — this is the shell date and the Batangas GLA add. Decide whether holiday and Q4 traffic feeds those malls or stays neighborhood/commissary. Don’t ignore General Trias in the next-year list if Cavite traffic matters to your map.
