Chef Miggy Moreno builds on Michelin recognition with Palm Grill expansion

4 Nov 2026 · ABS-CBN Lifestyle

Palm Grill, the Tomas Morato restaurant serving Zamboanga and Sulu dishes, opens at Gateway Mall in Araneta City by mid-November. It moves there from a corner of the now-closed Palenque and adds to its NAIA Terminal 3 branch. Moreno is also preparing a branch at SM City Zamboanga. Palm Grill and his other restaurant, Cabel, both kept their Bib Gourmand in the 2027 Michelin Guide selection announced 29 Oct. Beyond his restaurants, he now sells ready-to-eat piyanggang manok and beef kurma through 7-Eleven Philippines, in Luzon from 30 Oct and in Mindanao from 2 Dec. He has also worked with hotel kitchens including Manila Marriott, Admiral Hotel, NUSTAR Cebu and Crimson Boracay.

Operator read: Moreno opens new stores one at a time and in places that already get foot traffic: an airport, a mall that replaced a closed food hall, and his hometown mall. Meanwhile the 7-Eleven deal puts his name in front of shoppers across the country while someone else carries the inventory. A recipe that holds up as a packaged meal is a product you can license. If one of your signature dishes could survive a cold chain, the companies running convenience stores and hotel kitchens are worth a call before you sign another lease. Regional food is also still a gap in the Manila market, so if you want a concept that's different from everyone else, look there.

Could BonChon thrive in the Philippines once again?

3 Nov 2026 · Radar

Thai restaurant group Minor Food completed its acquisition of BonChon, the South Korean fried chicken chain, from Serruya Private Equity in September. It had already run more than 100 BonChon outlets in Thailand since 2019. Minor Food wants 4,500 outlets by 2029, with BonChon as the main brand it grows worldwide, and it lists the Philippines as a key market for expansion. Radar notes that Filipino customers have complained in recent years about product quality, sanitation and smaller servings while prices kept rising.

Operator read: a new owner with a big outlet target usually means a lot of new stores and a fresh marketing push, so expect BonChon to bid for mall space and run promos that hit chicken prices across the market. If your chicken competes with it, it's a good month to lock down what you do best and protect your portion sizes. The bigger lesson is in the complaints. Customers notice when the plate gets smaller, and once the brand is known for it, the new owner has to spend to win them back.
