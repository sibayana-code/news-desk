PROMO: Jollibee puts fillet on the ₱99 floor

9 Sep 2026

Jollibee is running three Pepper Cream Chicken Fillet meal combinations at ₱99 from Sep 9 to 24: fillet + fries and drink (was ₱130), fillet + Jolly Spaghetti (was ₱145), and fillet + Coke Float (was ₱154) — savings of ₱31 to ₱55. Dine-in, take-out, and drive-thru at participating stores, while supplies last. Marketing VP Dorothy Ching framed it as variety under ₱100; the operator read is simpler: McD already pledged to keep Sulit Busog at ₱99, and Jollibee is meeting that floor with a non-Chickenjoy SKU. Flag this as promo — two weeks, not a permanent price list. COO/opener take: if your lunch ticket sits above ₱120 without a clear “why pay more,” the majors will own the price-sensitive day-part through late September. Either ship a value stack or lean harder into a protein/day-part they don’t sell.

Diesel past ₱100 — kitchen tax is back

8 Sep 2026

DOE’s Sep 8–14 adjustment: gasoline +₱4.69, diesel +₱5.18, kerosene +₱5.58 per liter after last week’s rollback. Energy Secretary Sharon Garin pinned it on Middle East flow stress plus a weaker peso. Some Metro Manila pumps now print over ₱100/L — gasoline up to ~₱102.04, premium diesel ~₱102.68. Rappler’s pre-hike NCR averages (Sep 1–7) sat near ₱73.60 RON91 and ₱85.60 diesel before the jump. Same week, PSA-CALABARZON (Sep 9) said regional Aug inflation eased to 5.6% from 5.8%, but Batangas rose to 4.6% from 4.2% on education, transport, and health — with diesel and gasoline nearly double year-ago in the briefing. Laguna and Cavite eased; Quezon and Lucena City rose. Kitchen read: this hits delivery radius, wet-market runs, and anything trucked from Batangas coast before LPG moves. Re-quote proteins and produce midweek; don’t write September food cost on last Monday’s sheets.

ALI spends again — south corridors stay live

8 Sep 2026

Ayala Corp. kept buying Ayala Land shares through the slump while ALI traded near multi-year lows (~₱15). The developer raised its 2026 capex budget to about ₱60 billion after cutting hard when the Middle East war hit an already soft, oversupplied housing market. Insider PH’s Ron Acoba read: parent accumulation at depressed valuations as management turns constructive again. Parallel signal: Laurean Residences (₱28B Makati tower on hold with >₱10B booked) — 50% of buyers chose to wait rather than refund or switch, per CEO Meean Dy; restart decision only in 2027. Opener/COO read: ignore the ticker for catchment. ALI already leased Makro into Broadfield (Biñan), Evo City (Kawit), Cloverleaf, and Arca South. Capex back on means those south Luzon estates keep densifying — grocery anchors, HORECA volume, and lease asks follow the spend. If you’re Calamba-adjacent, map Broadfield and Nuvali traffic before someone else owns the lunch and catering lane.
