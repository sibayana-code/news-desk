Samgyupsalamat hits 100 stores just as pork shoulder gets cheaper, while special rice costs 12% more than a year ago

Tape

  • National prices · PSA, 1–5 Nov: special rice ₱64.30/kg (₱57.33 a year ago), pork kasim ₱318.46/kg (down from ₱324.08 in late October and ₱348.21 a year ago). Carrots jumped to ₱149.52/kg from ₱134.72. Red onion ₱148.85, calamansi ₱90.59 and galunggong ₱241.73 also rose. (Manila Times, 18 Nov)
  • NCR prices · DA monitoring for 16 Nov: pork kasim ₱301.89/kg (down 1.4% in a week), whole chicken ₱210.09/kg (down 2.3%), local garlic ₱395/kg (up 4.3%), red onion ₱128.37/kg (up 2.8%), calamansi ₱114.03/kg (up 3.0%). Local well-milled rice ₱49.07/kg. (eAgri, DA data)
  • Mall rollout · Taco Bell, run locally by PPI Holdings, opened at Market! Market! in Taguig right after SM Fairview, next to a newly upgraded Dairy Queen. (Wazzup Pilipinas, 18 Nov · Flag: promo-style launch post)

Takes

  • Pork shoulder is down about ₱30/kg from a year ago. That's good news for unli-grill and pork-heavy menus going into December. Lock in a supplier price for the holidays now, before party demand pushes it back up.
  • Special rice is up 12% in a year while regular rice holds near ₱49/kg in NCR. If you serve unli rice or premium rice, check which grade you're using and price the refill separately if you need to.
  • Taco Bell placed itself next to its own sister brand, Dairy Queen, like Bistro's two-brand Denny's and SIKLAB+ store. If you run more than one brand, put them side by side so you get both the meal and the dessert sale.

Samgyupsalamat's 100th table is set in McKinley Hill

18 Nov 2026 · Haus Guest

The Korean barbecue chain scheduled its 100th-store celebration for 18 Nov at Venice Grand Canal Mall in Taguig, on the second floor near the Milano Street entrance. The date comes from an organizer's event listing. The listing gives no opening hours, per-person rate, reservation policy or opening promo, so the article says the date doesn't prove the store is already serving.

Operator read: 100 stores shows samgyup isn't just a fad. It's an established category, and it runs on pork belly and shared tables. With PSA showing pork shoulder ₱30/kg cheaper than last year, unli-grill margins have more room this December. For an opener in Laguna: the category is crowded, so don't copy the format. Compete on a group-dining price that fits your catchment, or on a protein samgyup doesn't sell.

Special rice price up, pork shoulder down

18 Nov 2026 · The Manila Times (PSA data)

PSA's early-November survey put special rice at ₱64.30/kg nationwide, up from ₱64.07 in late October and ₱57.33 a year earlier. Fresh pork kasim fell to ₱318.46/kg from ₱324.08 and ₱348.21 a year earlier. Carrots, red onions, calamansi, galunggong and brown sugar rose from the previous period. Of those, only calamansi costs more than a year ago.

Operator read: your two biggest staples are moving in opposite directions. Pork is getting cheaper and premium rice is getting dearer. Before you set December prices, reprice rice-heavy combos and silog meals, and promote pork dishes. Watch calamansi and onion, which go into a lot of sauces and dips.
