Lemon Square opens its 150th store next to its own new Batangas plant, McDonald’s prices merienda at ₱59, and pork keeps easing into November

Tape

  • Daily Tribune (Oct 17) · Lemon Square opened its 150th store at The Outlets at LIMA Estate in Batangas. Parent Big E Food Corp. recently launched an 11.8-hectare plant in the Lipa–Malvar area, expected to employ at least 2,000 once fully running, alongside its Bulacan plant. LIMA hosts 200-plus companies and about 75,000 jobs.
  • Foodie PH · McDonald’s Snack & Match runs ₱59 solo or ₱79 with a side or float, with new limited-time Honey Butter McShaker Fries, Creamy BBQ Burger McDo and Creamy BBQ Crispy Chicken Sandwich since Oct 1, on every channel including aggregators. Flag: brand release.
  • PSA via Manila Times (Oct 19) · Early-October retail: fresh pork ₱290.85/kg, down from ₱295.58 in mid-September and ₱323.58 a year ago. Eggplant ₱104.41 from ₱111.83, refined sugar ₱82.29, tilapia ₱185.27. Carabao mango was the only riser, at ₱198.97 from ₱189.41.
  • SunStar Davao (Oct 16) · Gerb Golden Hands Corp. of Sto. Tomas, Davao del Norte shipped 2.4 tonnes of saba to Hong Kong on Oct 13, with 3 more tonnes booked the next day, off a deal struck at Asia Fruit Logistica Hong Kong.
  • World Coffee Portal via franchise-association.org (Oct 8–10) · Gong cha extended its Philippine master franchise by 10 years and plans 50 new stores over that decade, on top of 170-plus open since its 2011 start at SM Mall of Asia.
  • PAGASA (Oct 18–19) · No cyclone inside PAR. A low-pressure area may enter late this week but isn’t expected to change the weather much. Early-morning thunderstorm advisory covered Calamba, Cabuyao and Santa Rosa.

Takes

  • Lemon Square put a store in the same estate as its plant, where 75,000 people already clock in. That’s the cheapest catchment there is: guaranteed weekday traffic, shift breaks you can schedule around, and your own staff as walk-ins. If you’re scouting south of Calamba, rank sites by headcount and shift times in LIMA, Carmelray and LISP, not by census population. Open
  • McDonald’s at ₱59 for the 3 pm slot, Mang Inasal at ₱89 for lunch, Jollibee at ₱99: the big chains now have every daypart covered under ₱100. Don’t fight that at lunch. Fill your own dead afternoon hours with a ₱59–₱79 merienda pair built only from what’s already on the fryer and the drink station, so it adds no new stock.
  • Pork is down about ₱33/kg year on year and still sliding, and storms are quiet. This is the moment to lock pork and vegetable prices through December, before holiday buying lifts them and the El Niño squeeze arrives in January. Mango is the one going the other way, so move December desserts off fresh carabao mango.

Lemon Square plants store number 150 beside its own new factory in LIMA Estate

17 Oct 2026

Daily Tribune: Lemon Square opened its 150th store at The Outlets at LIMA Estate, the Aboitiz Economic Estates development in Batangas. Parent Big E Food Corp. recently launched a manufacturing plant in the Lipa–Malvar area covering 118,132 square meters, or about 11.8 hectares. It’s expected to generate at least 2,000 jobs once fully operational, and it will back up the existing Bulacan plant for the local market and the wider region. Technical director Ernesto Ramos Jr. framed the store as making products more accessible to families around the estate. Aboitiz VP Clifford Academia said it shows how manufacturing and retail can support each other inside one development. LIMA has more than 200 locators and about 75,000 jobs, and it has expanded into offices, retail, housing and schools.

Operator read: this is a snack-bakery brand moving production closer to the fast-growing south and selling right where the workers are. For a Calamba-based opener, two things follow. First, Batangas and southern Laguna are getting more consumer-goods plants, which means more shift workers with steady pay and fixed break times, and those are the easiest diners to forecast. Second, estate outlet strips like The Outlets at LIMA are landlords that want food tenants to keep the workforce on site. Ask for traffic counts by shift and by gate before you look at the rent. Flag: company and estate statements at a ribbon-cutting, no sales or plant output figures.
