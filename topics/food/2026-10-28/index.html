DTI: no price increase on basic necessities and prime commodities until Dec 31

27 Oct 2026

ABS-CBN News reports Trade Secretary Ma. Cristina Roque said prices of the basic necessities and prime commodities DTI monitors will stay stable through December 31. “For now, no price increase po for the basic necessities and prime commodities until December 31, 2026,” she said, adding that supply is ample and there is no need for panic buying or hoarding. DTI continues weekly checks in supermarkets and groceries to keep covered products within suggested retail prices.

Operator read: this is a holiday shelf hold, not a cost freeze for kitchens. It keeps consumer staples flat on the grocery side, which makes ₱99–₱150 value meals look a little less like a bargain next to home cooking in December. On the buy side, it gives you a talking point: if manufacturers can hold SRPs to year-end, your distributors can hold dry-goods quotes too. Fresh pork, chicken and vegetables are outside this and still need their own locks.

Japan’s Katsuya pork-cutlet chain opens its first Philippine store, betting on Southeast Asia

27 Oct 2026

Nikkei Asia reports that Arclands, the operator of tonkatsu chain Katsuya, opened its first Philippine location in Metro Manila in late September. The company is seeking growth overseas, with an eye on Southeast Asia, as its domestic operations in Japan struggle. (Nikkei’s full article is paywalled; the details here are from its headline, summary and lede.)

Operator read: Japanese chains keep treating Manila as the overflow market, and the ones coming now are the value formats, not the premium ones. For a casual-dining operator that means a sharper set price on katsu and rice bowls in the malls. For an opener, it is also a franchise signal: a parent that needs foreign growth is more likely to talk master franchise for the provinces. Laguna and Cavite malls are the logical next stop once Metro Manila is proven.
