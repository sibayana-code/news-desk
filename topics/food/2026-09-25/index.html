Michelin Guide PH 2027: Oct 29 at Marriott Newport

24 Sep 2026

Insider PH / Manila Times: The MICHELIN Guide will unveil its second restaurant selection for Manila and Environs and Cebu on Oct 29 at the Manila Marriott Hotel, Newport World Resorts — invitation-only, livestreamed on the MICHELIN Guide Asia YouTube channel. The 2027 edition sits in the Star system’s 100th anniversary year (introduced 1926). Awards on the night: One / Two / Three Stars, Bib Gourmand (good cooking at good value), and MICHELIN Selected. Inspectors use five universal criteria: ingredient quality, cooking technique, flavor harmony, cuisine personality, and consistency over time and across the menu. Ceremony F&B will come from award-winning local and international chefs. Operator read for Calamba: you are not chasing Stars from a commissary kitchen. Steal Bib Gourmand — a price band and consistency bar guests can feel. South Luzon traffic still splits Nuvali/SM clocks and wet-market rice near ₱50; a “good value, good cooking” promise is the mid-ticket lane that survives when QSR is fighting memory bundles and Mega Meals. Mark Oct 29 on the calendar for which Manila/Cebu concepts get the halo, then ask which of those formats could franchise or inspire a Laguna plate — not which chef dinner you attend.

PROMO Day after ₱99 fillet: Forgotten Island vs McD fill

25 Sep 2026

Jollibee’s two-week ₱99 Pepper Cream Chicken Fillet combos ended yesterday (Sep 24). Still live and flagged as promo: Forgotten Island Adventure Bundle through Oct 31 nationwide — 1-pc Chickenjoy with rice and Pineapple Quencher, Peach Mango Pie à la Mode, Peach Mango Pie with chocolate sauce, Friendship Charm Bracelet, ₱389 — tied to DreamWorks’ Filipino-mythology film. On the other side of the street, McDonald’s has not left a vacuum: Simpol (Sep 2) has Mega Meals from ₱195 since Aug 14 (Chicken McDo + second protein + drink + Plain Sundae; dual-protein without sundae from ₱158; classic 1-pc with rice and drink from ₱99). Freeman (Sep 8): McD PH is in global top-10 by store count and top-5 for growth; Cebu adds five more doors before year-end (SRP Talisay, Casamira Guadalupe, Yati Liloan, Compostela, ICM relocate) on top of Marigondon and Tipolo already open; VisMin is only ~one-fifth of 862 PH stores. Cost lever: Corenergy power deal on 17 Cebu restaurants aiming 10–14% savings — utilities are 6–7% of sales — to keep the value floor. Operator read: this morning is the real chicken split. JFC sells friendship memory after the savings sticker dies; McD sells fill and a defended ₱99 rice plate while it densifies VisMin. If you plate chicken in Calamba this weekend, answer one question on the board — memory, heat, or fill — and price it. Don’t try to out-bracelet Jollibee and out-Mega McD with one SKU.

Nestlé backs DA’s CIDO: Kapeng Pilipino sourcing push

16 Sep 2026

LionhearTV / Nestlé / DA: Agriculture Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel Jr.’s Department Order No. 06 created the Coffee Industry Development Office (CIDO) under the Office of the Undersecretary for Special Concerns and ODA (Jerome Oliveros) — a dedicated desk to centralize coffee programs, policy and budget, lift farmer productivity, and cut import dependence. Nestlé Philippines, the country’s biggest buyer of local coffee and biggest local manufacturer, publicly backed the office and the “Kapeng Pilipino para sa Pilipino” line. Through the NESCAFÉ Plan it keeps running productivity training, regenerative agriculture and sustainable sourcing, and is meeting CIDO on partnership areas. Nestlé Chairman/CEO Mauricio Alarcon and corporate affairs leadership sat with Oliveros and Asec. Arnel de Mesa on the collaboration. Operator read: this is agri-export and beverage sourcing in one move. Rome FAO Hand-in-Hand in October already has coffee on DA’s pitch list beside mango, seaweed and cacao. For a Calamba opener with coffee on the board, the steal is Nestlé’s structure — local kg share tied to a government desk and a farmer program — not a green sticker without a PO. Watch whether CIDO money and Nestlé offtake actually tighten local green-bean supply; if they do, espresso and cold-brew costs move before the slogan does.
