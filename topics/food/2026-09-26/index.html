PAGASA: Very Strong El Niño into H1 2027

23 Sep 2026

DOST-PAGASA (Sep 23): a Strong El Niño is already active and is expected to intensify to Very Strong between September and December 2026, then persist through the first half of 2027. The bureau flags prolonged dry spells and drought risk for agriculture, water, energy and food security — while warning that Habagat and cyclones can still dump localized heavy rain on the western Philippines even as the basin runs drier overall. Philstar / DA (Sep 10): spokesperson Arnel de Mesa says this year’s rice supply is not the El Niño story; the full brunt lands on the dry-season harvest in H1 2027. Policy stance: keep pushing local production and do not stop imports to build stocks — rice imports already 3.67 million MT as of Sep 3, with a path toward ~5 million MT (2024 record was 4.81). Asks on the table: ₱6.2B more for UPLIFT fuel/fertilizer and ₱5B for solar irrigation. 2024 El Niño left ₱15.3B in agri damage and a 3.3% output drop. Operator read for Calamba: regular milled rice has already been flirting with ₱50 in PSA early-September prints, but the forward fight is drought on palay next dry season plus power and veg stress. Do not rewrite the weekend board. Do lock rice and produce suppliers with drought-aware lead times and alternate SKUs before H1 2027 — and treat utilities as a line item that can move with El Niño, the same way McD Cebu treated Corenergy to defend a ₱99 floor.

MerryMart + NAOS live at Nuvali Central Bloc

22 Sep 2026

Ayala Land Estates (Sep 22): MerryMart and NAOS Café are open at The Shops NUVALI at Central Bloc — grocery, fresh/frozen, pharmacy and household essentials under one roof, café/co-working attached, daily 8am–8pm. Ribbon cut with DoubleDragon/MerryMart and NAOS brass beside Ayala Land Estates. Gilbert Ramos (Senior Project Development Manager): every opening is about a “more complete and connected estate.” Still coming in Q4 at the same block: Yuimaru, Seoul Kitchen, Locals Coffee, Nuat Thai Premier, Nailandia Luxe, Harinaya, plus barber/salon/dental/pilates. Weekend Markets keep a lane for NUVALI Locals bakers and food makers. This sits inside Ayala’s Rising South story south of Metro Manila, while SM Nuvali still targets Nov 27 on Santa Rosa–Tagaytay Road with a one-hectare indoor garden and dining expected over 30% of tenants. Operator read: Calamba catchment is being grocery-densified now and dining-layered before Christmas. MerryMart is the daily trip; Seoul Kitchen / Yuimaru / Locals Coffee are the mid-ticket stack that will steal lunch and coffee before SM’s garden halo lands. If you open or replate near Nuvali this quarter, answer grocery adjacency and weekday coffee/lunch first — not a weekend destination concept that waits for the mall ribbon.

RRHI ber months: food + drugstore outside NCR

21 Sep 2026

Philstar (Sep 21): Robinsons Retail Holdings Inc. president/CEO Stanley Co says openings skew to the ber months and the pipeline favors banners with resilient demand and low penetration — especially food and drugstores. CapEx for 2026 stays ₱5–7B; network already >2,700 company stores plus >2,100 franchised TGP. Co is “particularly positive” on food and drugstore essentials, still open to M&A, and still investing in discretionary formats’ value proposition. Fresh proof point: first Robinsons Supermarket in Panglao, Bohol (opened Sep 4 per Rappler), paired with Rose Pharmacy’s ninth Bohol door at Bellemar Lifestyle Center — pitched at residents and tourism workers, hotels, resorts and restaurants that need regular grocery and fresh. Rappler’s end-2025 map: of 799 food-segment stores (Robinsons Supermarket, Marketplace, Shopwise, Easymart, Uncle John’s), 410 sat in Metro Manila, 308 elsewhere in Luzon, only 56 Visayas and 25 Mindanao. Food was 59.8% of RRHI revenue in 2025 (₱125.8B), with 3.2% same-store sales growth. Shares left the PSE Aug 31 after voluntary delisting. Operator read: the Gokongwei food machine is hunting tourism and provincial catchments where modern trade is thin, not stacking another NCR box. For a Calamba opener the steal is the Panglao logic — serve the workers and kitchens around a leisure corridor, not only the weekend tourist — and watch whether South Luzon’s next supermarket/drugstore densification (Nuvali MerryMart already live; SM Nuvali Nov 27) compresses wet-market and sari-sari share the same way RRHI is betting VisMin still has room.
