Operators and catchment

Tape

Yesterday was rice and storms. Today the operators are moving. McD is locking regional chicken as it punches toward 900 stores. Jollibee is still buying the weekend drive-thru visit with a P149 pair, not a menu-wide cut. Gokongwei takes the supermarket group private Monday.

For Calamba, ALI is putting more households into Nuvali, Lipa, and now Tarlac Cresendo. The mall F&B list next door is branded-up. That’s new rooftops and new rent, not a reason to sit in-line next to Chili’s.

Takes

  • Chicken: a 900-store QSR is pulling regional growers onto its spec. If you fry chicken, your poultry quote is competing with that buyer. Dual-source now. Open
  • Price: match the traffic play on one cheap SKU (weekend, drive-thru), not the whole card. Jollibee is not cutting Chickenjoy to do this. Open
  • Site: hunt estate-edge and CALAX/SLEX service, not a Nuvali in-line. Households are coming. The mall slots will go to chains. Broadfield plus Makro is the wholesale/traffic tell for Laguna. Open
  • Grocery: RRHI private means Robinsons Supermarket / Marketplace can move format and private-label without a public print. Watch the shelf, not the ticker. Open
