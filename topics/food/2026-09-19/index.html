PSA: rice near ₱50/kg, cabbage +37% — early-Sep kitchen print

18 Sep 2026

SunStar / PSA: The biweekly price situationer for Sep 1–5 (released Sep 18) shows six of seven monitored agri commodities up versus early August. Regular milled rice averaged ₱49.96/kg — a few centavos above August’s ₱49.64 / ₱49.61, but ₱9.65 (+24%) above the same window in 2025 (₱40.31). Cabbage led the month-to-month jump at ₱125.86/kg from ₱91.63 early August (~+37%). Carabao mango ₱182.96 (+17% vs early Aug), galunggong ₱248.53, native garlic ₱173.36, chicken egg ₱9.26/pc. Brown sugar was the lone soft print at ₱71.55. Separately, PSA standing-crop estimates as of Aug 1 put Q3 palay at ~3.19 MMT (−15% YoY from 3.75) and corn at ~2.08 MMT (−14.4% from 2.43), with planted area down double digits. Kitchen take for Calamba: storm-veg pressure from last week’s DA ₱4.38B damage tape is now sitting beside a hard national retail print. Rice is the sticky cost — flat MoM but still a year-ago shock — so rice-meal combos and staff meals need a live quote, not a menu card written in July. Dual-source cabbage and lean specials until the second-phase (15–17 Sep) print lands.

SM Nuvali opens Nov 27 — Laguna flagship with a 1-ha indoor garden

1–2 Sep 2026

Rappler / GMA / BusinessWorld: SM Supermalls set Nov 27 for SM Nuvali in Santa Rosa, Laguna — about 82,000 sqm GLA, the group’s 91st Philippine mall and its fifth and largest in Laguna. Centerpiece is a roughly one-hectare indoor garden ringed by restaurants; president Steven Tan says a Gardens by the Bay–style two-layer membrane lets sunlight in while dumping trapped heat, so the garden itself is air-conditioned. Tan framed it as leaving “cookie-cutter” boxes for architecture that fits the local community, and called the South Luzon / CALABARZON corridor the growth bet. Same briefing: SM City Sto. Tomas (Batangas) expands GLA ~30% with a Green Haven (cinemas, coworking, wellness) targeted October; SM Iloilo +46,000 sqm; SM Naga two-story add; Tagum and General Trias eyed next year. H1 mall occupancy hit a record 96%; mall revenues +8% to ₱41.8B. COO/opener read: this is the Ayala-lots-style catchment story for Calamba — Santa Rosa–Tagaytay Road, not BGC. When an 82k-sqm lifestyle box with restaurant-facing garden opens before Christmas, weekend FOHO and family traffic re-center. Decide now whether you want a mall door, a corridor spillover site, or a deliberate stay-away from the food court fight.

JFC picks Hong Kong for JFCI spinoff — PH stays on PSE

1 Sep 2026

Jollibee Group / Philstar / BusinessWorld: JFC advanced its plan to separate international ops into Jollibee Foods Corporation International (JFCI) and is contemplating a Main Board listing on HKEX instead of the earlier US path. JFCI holds the current international business; JFC remains PSE-listed with Philippine operations. Richard Chong Woo Shin (JFC CFO/Risk Officer and JFC International CEO) is named JFCI CEO, full-time after separation. Chairman Tony Tan Caktiong: detailed work since the Jan 6 listing plan reinforced conviction that Hong Kong fits JFCI’s Asia-heavy footprint and investor set. Existing JFC shareholders are expected to receive JFCI shares proportional to their interest, subject to taxes and rules. Still contingent on restructuring, diligence, market conditions, and approvals including the HKEX Listing Committee — no assurance on timing or completion. Operator take: this is the food-group corporate move on the Figaro/Angel’s Pizza template — two listed vehicles, clearer capital stories. For PH franchisees and rivals, the near-term signal is whether the domestic company, once stripped of overseas growth narrative, runs tighter on promo spend and denser on provincial stores. Watch guidance and capex on the PH side after the papers clear, not the Hong Kong ticker drama.
