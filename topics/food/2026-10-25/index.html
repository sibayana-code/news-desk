Pork holiday runs into Sunday as DA lines up a tariff hike and an import freeze

23–24 Oct 2026

ABS-CBN News: about 1,000 hog raisers protested outside the Department of Agriculture on Friday and extended their pork holiday, which started Thursday, through Sunday. They want a total import ban. ProPork’s Nonon Tabago says Cebu farmgate prices average ₱120 a kilo while it costs ₱180–190 to raise one, a loss of ₱5,000–9,000 a head. GMA News reports the DA answered on Saturday: it wants the tariff on imported pork back at 40% and a two-month import holiday, and is preparing a general safeguards investigation. None of it is final; the tariff needs the Tariff Commission and the President. Philstar quotes Secretary Tiu Laurel that permits for imported kasim, pigue and liempo stop by mid-November for 60 days. DA says the country holds about 170 days of pork stock, so it does not expect a Christmas price spike.

Operator read: the short-term risk is small, since the holiday mostly thins fresh pork in some wet markets for a few days. The real risk is December. Imported liempo and kasim are the cuts behind sisig, lechon kawali, BBQ and most value bowls, and the freeze targets exactly those. Importers sitting on stock will reprice the moment permits stop, so get December and January volumes and prices in writing now. The flip side is local pork: farmgate is below cost and raisers need buyers, which makes this a good week to approach a Laguna or Batangas hog supplier for a fixed-price contract. If a 40% tariff does pass in 2027, that contract is your hedge.

Promo watch: ₱10 add-ons, a ₱79 sundae and Chowking’s own app

24 Oct 2026 · Promo, flagged

ABS-CBN Lifestyle’s weekly roundup, built largely on brand handouts: Pizza Hut is selling its Four Cheese Baked Mac, normally ₱249, for ₱10 with any large pan or stuffed-crust pizza, Monday to Thursday until November 5. Jollibee’s ₱79 Pistachio Choco Sundae, made with Dubai-based Filipino pastry chef Nouel Catis, runs through December 31. Mang Inasal holds its Ihaw Fest with Coco Martin today at Ayala Malls Feliz, with passes earned through October receipts. Chowking has launched a mobile app with scheduled orders, pickup, QR ordering and lower delivery fees.

Operator read: the Pizza Hut deal is a weekday-traffic play, not a discount on the hero product. It protects the pizza price and uses a cheap-to-make side to fill slow days. That structure is worth copying for Monday to Thursday. Chowking’s app matters more than the sundae: once the big groups own the order, they own the customer data and skip aggregator commissions. If you run delivery, track what share comes direct and set a target to raise it.
