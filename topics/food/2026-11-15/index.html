'Higpit-sinturon': Pasig shoppers feel pinch of rising vegetable prices

14 Nov 2026 · ABS-CBN News

A market check in Pasig found carrots and potatoes at ₱140/kg each, up from ₱100–120 last month. Tomatoes and calamansi are at ₱160/kg and cabbage at ₱200. Green chili, lemon, Baguio beans and patola rose to ₱240/kg from ₱200. Some items got cheaper: Baguio pechay and radish fell to ₱160 from ₱240, ginger to ₱200 from ₱240, and kangkong and pechay to ₱10 a bundle. Onions and garlic are at ₱140/kg.

Operator read: root crops and salad and garnish items are going up, while leafy greens are going down. Look at your recipes for the ingredients you can't swap out (potato sides, slaw cabbage, calamansi) and decide now whether to absorb the cost or change the portions. Push leafy-green dishes on the holiday menu while they're cheap, and get a fixed December price from your supplier before the holiday rush pushes the wet market up further.

7-Eleven partners with Palm Grill's Chef Miggy Moreno to bring Southern Mindanao flavors to more Filipinos

Nov 2026 · LionhearTV

7-Eleven Philippines' latest Chef Creations collaboration is with Palm Grill, the Michelin-recognized Tausug restaurant of Chef Miggy Moreno. Developed with Maggi Magic Meals, it turns Southern Mindanao dishes into ready-to-eat meals starting at ₱149. Chicken Pianggang and Beef Kurma went on sale in select Luzon stores from 30 Oct. Belachan Chicken and Sambal Istah come later, and select Mindanao stores will follow.

Operator read: this is a format play. A convenience-store chain is borrowing a chef's credibility, a supplier is funding the development, and the dishes are priced near a QSR value meal. For a chef or restaurant, a co-branded line like this is a way to get your name in front of more people without opening more stores. For a QSR, it means 7-Eleven is now competing for the ₱150 lunch with a better story. If you're thinking about something similar, make sure the deal includes a cut of every unit sold and an exit clause, not just a one-time fee.
