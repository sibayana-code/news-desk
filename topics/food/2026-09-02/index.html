JFC picks HKEX for the overseas spin-off

2 Sep 2026

Jollibee Foods told the PSE on Tuesday it is contemplating a listing of Jollibee Foods Corporation International (JFCI) on the HKEX main board, dropping the January plan to list overseas assets in the US. JFCI holds the businesses outside the Philippines; JFC stays on the PSE with the domestic book. Chairman Tony Tan Caktiong: Hong Kong is “best aligned with JFCI’s business, geographic footprint, and long-term ambitions.” Richard Chong Woo Shin (group CFO and risk officer, already CEO of JFC International) keeps both hats until the split, then JFCI CEO full-time. Shareholders as of a record date are expected to receive JFCI shares matching their JFC interest, subject to tax and approvals. Still needs restructuring, due diligence, market conditions, and HKEX Listing Committee clearance. No assurance on terms or timing. JFC closed Tuesday at P152.80, up P2.80.

2 Sep 2026

McDonald’s PH briefed on Chicken McDo “Gold Standards” (look, texture, flavor, freshness, food safety) and ~3,200 restaurant Chicken Experts who check thaw, cook quantity, and dump anything that misses the spec. [promo] Mega Meals (from August) start at P195: Chicken McDo plus a second protein, rice, drink, Plain Sundae. Lower-priced protein meal from P158. Taro Custard Pie joins Mega Meals from 14 Sep. Sulit vs Jollibee is still the frame; the operator story is the SOP and the local bird.

Rice: buffer in, harvest on, no import ban

2 Sep 2026

BPI: 3.46 million MT of rice in as of Aug 13, already past the 3.39 million MT imported in all of 2025. Vietnam 2.59M, Thailand 378k, Myanmar 286k. SPSICs issued for 5.35 million MT as of Aug 12. Agriculture Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel Jr. told a briefing yesterday the country had imported 3.8 million tons as of last week, harvest has started, prices “maayos,” and supply should carry through end-2026. No import ban into El Niño (buffer for Dec–Apr 2027). Palay output this year may be 18.6–18.8 million MT, about 700k MT lighter. Farmer groups still want a 30% safeguard. For a kitchen: the quote is a Q1 2027 problem, not a September shortage.

JGF × UPLB: farmer pipeline next door

2 Sep 2026

Jollibee Group Foundation signed an MOU with UP Los Baños covering farmer training, tech transfer, and Agri-Tech Facilitators (community extension workers). JGF president Gisela Tiongson with UPLB chancellor Jose Camacho Jr. Farmer Entrepreneurship Program: more than 1,800 smallholders in 12 provinces, collectively more than 20 million kilos of produce and over P1 billion in farmer income. The new work names green bell pepper as a crop for tech and capacity-building. UPLB is Los Baños. For a Calamba kitchen that is a sourcing conversation, not a CSR clipping.

Davao adds chayote and camote to Jeddah

2 Sep 2026

DA-11 and GERB Golden Hands Corp. sent off another Jeddah box on 26 Aug from Sto. Tomas, Davao del Norte: chayote, cardava banana, sweet potato, singkamas, for Rana Mohammed Saleem Commercial LLC. Builds on GERB’s July shipment (17.2 MT cardava and singkamas, about P800k). Link started at AGRA Middle East 2024 in Dubai. DA-11 director Macario Gonzaga: vegetables this time, “chayote, which Davao Region is very famous for.” GERB CEO Dolores Lazarus wants the lane to hold with BPI help. Farmgate vs the P46.50/kg export average is still the open question — volume leaving Davao is not the same as money in the farmer’s pocket.
