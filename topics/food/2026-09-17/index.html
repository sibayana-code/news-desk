Jollibee puts Pepper Cream Fillet combos at ₱99 through Sep 24

9–24 Sep 2026

Jollibee PH: Three limited combos — 1-pc Pepper Cream Chicken Fillet with Fries and Drink (was ₱130), with Jolly Spaghetti (was ₱145), or with Coke Float (was ₱154) — all ticketed at ₱99 from Sep 9 to 24 at participating stores for dine-in, take-out, and drive-thru while supplies last. Marketing VP Dorothy Ching frames it as variety inside a sub-₱100 meal and savings up to ₱55. COO read: this is the same price-war lane as McD’s ₱99 Sulit Busog. Guests will compare tickets; operators should compare contribution after promo volume, not just the headline peso. If your Laguna ticket still “absorbs” diesel and Meralco to match ₱99, you are funding the guest — find the lever before you match the number.

Goldilocks eyes up to 100 openings — domestic toward ~1,100

24 Aug 2026

BusinessWorld: Goldilocks Bakeshop (SM Group) still explores near-100 openings a year; COO Jerson Go Uy put executable adds at 50–100, with close to 30 already open by mid-August and about half of the new slate franchised. Domestic network is projected near 1,100 by year-end after the 1,000th late last year. Coverage includes harder provinces (Siquijor, Bohol). No new international stores this cycle — export is the overseas bet. SM mall synergies help access, without guaranteed preferential lease treatment. Opener take: franchise-backed bakery density is a different threat than QSR tickets — it changes merienda and celebration share of stomach across provinces, not only SM food courts.

McD’s VisMin map still the ₱99 defense case study

8 Sep 2026

Philstar / The Freeman: McDonald’s Philippines keeps Visayas–Mindanao as the growth engine — Cebu at 38 stores with five more by YE (SRP Talisay, Casamira Guadalupe, Yati Liloan, Compostela, ICM relocate) after Marigondon and Tipolo this year. National network ~862; Visayas ~100 and Mindanao ~75 still about one-fifth. Utilities ~6–7% of sales (electricity ~72% of that); COREnergy / RAP expansion is the power lever behind the public vow to hold ₱99 Sulit Busog. Same desk as yesterday’s Food, still live: if Jollibee is answering at ₱99 on fillet, McD’s answer remains density plus kilowatt-hours, not a plate-price race to the bottom.
