Rice up 22% YoY

PSA: regular milled rice averaged P49.61/kg on Aug 1–5, up 22.16% from P40.61 a year ago. A touch above early-July P49.32. Main harvest still starts September.

Tape

Rice is the cost problem that does not wait for harvest. You are paying about P9/kg more than last August on regular, storms already chewed vegetables in the Luzon belts you buy from, and BSP is telling you August inflation stays hot on food. Pork is the one line that actually got cheaper. Use that.

September harvest and open imports should stop a spike, not unwind the last twelve months. El Niño from November is the next supply risk, including poultry. Treat rice and veg as tight through Q4, and do not let a cheaper pork invoice talk you into ignoring the rest of the basket.

Takes

  • Protect rice-heavy plates (silog, extra rice, porridge). Push pork while bone-in is ~10% cheaper YoY. Watch galunggong and cooking oil.
  • Dual-source vegetables away from the eight hit regions for the next couple of weeks. Lock oil and dry stores before the next weather print.
  • Guests feel rice. Do not raise the whole menu. Put the increase on rice add-ons and oil-heavy fry items.
  • Site: storm-hit Central Luzon is stressed demand, not a new catchment. Near-term traffic is still Metro malls.
