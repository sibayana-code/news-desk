Reading of the day

A reading from the Letter of St. Paul to the Ephesians 6:10-20

Brothers and sisters: Draw your strength from the Lord and from his mighty power. Put on the armor of God so that you may be able to stand firm against the tactics of the Devil. For our struggle is not with flesh and blood but with the principalities, with the powers, with the world rulers of this present darkness, with the evil spirits in the heavens. Therefore, put on the armor of God, that you may be able to resist on the evil day and, having done everything, to hold your ground. So stand fast with your loins girded in truth, clothed with righteousness as a breastplate, and your feet shod in readiness for the Gospel of peace. In all circumstances, hold faith as a shield, to quench all the flaming arrows of the Evil One. And take the helmet of salvation and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God.

With all prayer and supplication, pray at every opportunity in the Spirit. To that end, be watchful with all perseverance and supplication for all the holy ones and also for me, that speech may be given me to open my mouth, to make known with boldness the mystery of the Gospel for which I am an ambassador in chains, so that I may have the courage to speak as I must.

Gospel of the day

From the Gospel according to Luke 13:31-35

Some Pharisees came to Jesus and said, “Go away, leave this area because Herod wants to kill you.” He replied, “Go and tell that fox, ‘Behold, I cast out demons and I perform healings today and tomorrow, and on the third day I accomplish my purpose. Yet I must continue on my way today, tomorrow, and the following day, for it is impossible that a prophet should die outside of Jerusalem.’

“Jerusalem, Jerusalem, you who kill the prophets and stone those sent to you, how many times I yearned to gather your children together as a hen gathers her brood under her wings, but you were unwilling! Behold, your house will be abandoned. But I tell you, you will not see me until the time comes when you say, Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord.”

The words of the Popes

What must I defend myself against? What must I do? “Put on the armour of God”, Paul tells us; that is, what belongs to God defends us, so that we may resist the devil’s wiles. (…) But this generation – and so many others – has been led to believe that the devil is a myth, a figure, an idea, the idea of evil. But the devil exists and we must fight against him. Paul says so, not me! The Word of God says so. But we are not entirely convinced. And then Paul describes what this armour of God is like, what the various parts are that make up this great armour of God. And he says: “Stand therefore, having girded your loins with truth”. This is part of God’s armour: the truth. (…) The Christian life is a struggle, a beautiful struggle, because when the Lord triumphs at every stage of our lives, He gives us joy, great happiness: that joy that the Lord has brought about in us, through His freely-give gift of salvation. But yes, we are all a bit lazy, aren’t we, in the struggle, and we let ourselves be carried away by passion, by certain temptations. It is because we are all sinners! But do not lose heart. Take courage and be strong, for the Lord is with us. (Pope Francis, Homily at Santa Marta, 30 October 2014)

Word

armor /ˈɑːrmər/ — (noun) protective covering; figuratively, the spiritual defenses Paul names: truth, righteousness, readiness, faith, salvation, and the word of God.

From Old French armure, from Latin armatura “armor, equipment,” from armare “to arm.”

Aboitiz Foods strengthens supply chain as El Niño risks stretch into 2027

20 Oct 2026

Insider PH reports Aboitiz Foods is hardening supply: advance purchasing with suppliers, inventory buffers, water and feed-quality controls, and closer logistics coordination across eight countries. NDRRMC figures cited in the piece put El Niño impact at nearly 451,000 people and ₱6.5 million in agri losses so far; PAGASA expects conditions into early 2027. The company is developing a Pilmico feedmill at West Cebu Estate in Balamban, Cebu, to complement Iligan, and is working to reopen a Butuan meat-cutting facility aimed at fresh pork for restaurants, food businesses and households. Geopolitical freight and fuel costs, plus hog raisers squeezed when farmgate sits below cost, are flagged as parallel risks.

Operator read: this is mill-to-meal insurance, not a promo. If you buy fresh pork or use commercial feeders upstream of your suppliers, ask which plant and cut facility your quote actually rides on. A Cebu mill and a Butuan cut floor are Visayas–Mindanao capacity; Metro Manila operators still need their own NCR lock, but the signal is that big groups are stocking resilience before the dry stretch, not after prices spike.

Mang Inasal’s 13.2% sales lift keeps franchise interest high

19 Oct 2026

Insider PH reports Mang Inasal, with more than 600 stores, grew systemwide sales 13.2% in the first half of 2026 after a 15.6% full-year rise in 2025. Jollibee Foods Corp. said the run is still drawing franchise applicants. President Mike Castro credited Ihaw-Sarap Chicken Inasal and Juicy-lambot Pork BBQ, with takeout, delivery and drive-thru also in double-digit growth. Brand Finance’s 2026 QSR ranking named Mang Inasal the country’s strongest QSR brand (95.2, AAA+). JFC Philippines CEO Joseph Tanbuntiong said the parent is backing expansion through supply chain, operations and franchise development.

Operator read: grilled formats that hold ticket and throughput get the franchise money. That is more buyers for chicken quarters and BBQ cuts, and more charcoal/grill capacity competing for mall and roadside slots in Laguna and Cavite. Watch their marination/grilling process upgrades — consistency at 600+ stores is the playbook you either match or undercut on freshness.
