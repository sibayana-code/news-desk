Reading of the day

A reading from the Letter of St. Paul to the Ephesians 2:12-22

Brothers and sisters: You were at that time without Christ, alienated from the community of Israel and strangers to the covenants of promise, without hope and without God in the world. But now in Christ Jesus you who once were far off have become near by the Blood of Christ. For he is our peace, he made both one and broke down the dividing wall of enmity, through his Flesh, abolishing the law with its commandments and legal claims, that he might create in himself one new person in place of the two, thus establishing peace, and might reconcile both with God, in one Body, through the cross, putting that enmity to death by it. He came and preached peace to you who were far off and peace to those who were near, for through him we both have access in one Spirit to the Father. So then you are no longer strangers and sojourners, but you are fellow citizens with the holy ones and members of the household of God, built upon the foundation of the Apostles and prophets, with Christ Jesus himself as the capstone. Through him the whole structure is held together and grows into a temple sacred in the Lord; in him you also are being built together into a dwelling place of God in the Spirit.

Gospel of the day

From the Gospel according to Luke 12:35-38

Jesus said to his disciples: “Gird your loins and light your lamps and be like servants who await their master’s return from a wedding, ready to open immediately when he comes and knocks. Blessed are those servants whom the master finds vigilant on his arrival. Amen, I say to you, he will gird himself, have them recline at table, and proceed to wait on them. And should he come in the second or third watch and find them prepared in this way, blessed are those servants.”

The words of the Popes

Jesus says: “Let your loins be girded and your lamps burning” (Lk 12: 35). This means living fully in the present by grasping the promise of eternity. Our whole life is a striving for eternal life and we must be ready. A person is ready when he or she is completely given to God and to his or her brothers and sisters. Not when there is applause and success, no, life is much more than that. It is being in the world to the full, in the truth and freedom of the children of God and in the relationship of brotherhood with others. And this intensity of relationship with the Father and with our brothers and sisters is nourished by prayer: prayer allows God to be close to us, it frees us from solitude and infuses hope. Prayer oxygenates life: just as one cannot live without breathing, one cannot be a Christian and live as a Christian, let alone as a consecrated person, without prayer. (Pope Francis, Address to members of the Secular Institute of Oblate Missionary Cooperators, 20 November 2021)

Word

vigilant /ˈvɪdʒɪlənt/ — (adjective) keeping careful watch for possible danger or difficulty; alert and awake.

From Latin vigilans, present participle of vigilare “to keep awake, watch,” from vigil “awake, watchful.”

vigilant — Luke: blessed are the servants the master finds vigilant, loins girded and lamps lit, even in the second or third watch. Ephesians: those far off are brought near and built into God’s dwelling. Francis: we are ready when we are given to God and others, and prayer keeps that readiness alive. Mang Inasal keeps growing in double digits, and Jollibee is courting more franchisees

19 Oct 2026

Insider PH: Mang Inasal, which runs more than 600 stores nationwide, posted 13.2% systemwide sales growth in the first half of 2026, on top of 15.6% for the whole of 2025. Takeout, delivery and drive-thru grew at a double-digit pace, giving the chain more ways to reach customers outside its restaurants. Mang Inasal president Mike Castro credited the Chicken Inasal and pork barbecue. The company says it’s refining its marination and grilling so quality holds as the network grows. Brand Finance ranked it the Philippines’ strongest quick-service brand in 2026, at 95.2 out of 100 (AAA+). Parent Jollibee Foods Corp. said franchise interest keeps rising, and Jollibee Group Philippines CEO Joseph Tanbuntiong said the group’s supply chain and franchise development team will back the expansion.

Operator read: this is a recruitment pitch as much as a results story. Jollibee wants more franchisee money to fund Mang Inasal’s rollout, which means more grilled-chicken outlets landing in provincial malls and town centers, including the new Laguna and Batangas strips. If you run a chicken or rice-meal concept, assume a Mang Inasal opens near you within two years and compete where it’s weakest: dine-in comfort, local flavors, and the groups and occasions a ₱99 value set doesn’t serve. If you’re thinking of buying a franchise instead, ask for unit-level numbers by store age and the split between dine-in and off-premise, not the systemwide growth figure. Flag: company statement, no store-level or franchise economics disclosed.

Super Typhoon Champi nears PAR, where it will be named Rosal

20 Oct 2026

PAGASA’s 4 am bulletin put Super Typhoon Champi 1,605 km east of Central Luzon, with maximum winds of 195 km/h and gusts up to 240 km/h, moving west-northwest at 20 km/h. Its trough is already bringing scattered rain and thunderstorms to Catanduanes and Northern and Eastern Samar, and seas off eastern Luzon and the Visayas are moderate to rough. PAGASA’s threat outlook expects it to enter the northeastern part of PAR this week. OneNews.PH quoted PAGASA weather specialist Grace Castañeda saying its winds may be felt by Wednesday, Oct 21, with rain intensifying in the north later in the week. Metro Manila and the rest of Luzon only get isolated thunderstorms for now.

Operator read: the track isn’t fixed, and PAGASA lists recurving away from land as one common October path. But if it hits northern Luzon, Benguet highland vegetables are what move first, right as pork and vegetable prices had been easing. Don’t panic-buy. Do check this week how many days of cold storage you have, ask your vegetable supplier what they’re holding, and keep a sub list ready for cabbage, carrots and potatoes in case the price spikes.
