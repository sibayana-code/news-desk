Reading of the day

A reading from the First Letter of St. Paul to Corinthians 7:25-31

Brothers and sisters: In regard to virgins, I have no commandment from the Lord, but I give my opinion as one who by the Lord’s mercy is trustworthy. So this is what I think best because of the present distress: that it is a good thing for a person to remain as he is. Are you bound to a wife? Do not seek a separation. Are you free of a wife? Then do not look for a wife. If you marry, however, you do not sin, nor does an unmarried woman sin if she marries; but such people will experience affliction in their earthly life, and I would like to spare you that. I tell you, brothers, the time is running out. From now on, let those having wives act as not having them, those weeping as not weeping, those rejoicing as not rejoicing, those buying as not owning, those using the world as not using it fully. For the world in its present form is passing away.

Gospel of the day

From the Gospel according to Luke 6:20-26

Raising his eyes toward his disciples Jesus said:

“Blessed are you who are poor, for the Kingdom of God is yours. Blessed are you who are now hungry, for you will be satisfied. Blessed are you who are now weeping, for you will laugh. Blessed are you when people hate you, and when they exclude and insult you, and denounce your name as evil on account of the Son of Man.

Rejoice and leap for joy on that day! Behold, your reward will be great in heaven. For their ancestors treated the prophets in the same way.

But woe to you who are rich, for you have received your consolation. But woe to you who are filled now, for you will be hungry. Woe to you who laugh now, for you will grieve and weep. Woe to you when all speak well of you, for their ancestors treated the false prophets in this way.”

The words of the Popes

On the mountain, Christ gives his disciples the new law, no longer written on stone. It is a law that renews our lives and makes them good, even when the world seems to have failed us and is full of misery. Only God can truly call the poor and afflicted blessed (cf. vv. 3-4), because he is the highest good who gives himself to all with infinite love. Only God can satisfy those who seek peace and justice (cf. vv. 6.9), because he is the just judge of the world and the author of eternal peace. Only in God do the meek, the merciful and the pure of heart find joy (vv. 5.7-8), because he is the fulfilment of their expectations. In persecution, God is the source of redemption; in falsehood, he is the anchor of truth. Therefore, Jesus proclaims: “Rejoice and be glad!” (…) In this way, Jesus illuminates the meaning of history. It is no longer written by conquerors, but rather by God, who is able to accomplish it by saving the oppressed. (…) The Beatitudes become for us a measure of happiness, leading us to ask whether we consider it an achievement to be bought or a gift to be shared; whether we place it in objects that are consumed or in relationships that accompany us. It is in fact because of Christ (cf. Mt 5:11) and thanks to him that the bitterness of trials is transformed into the joy of the redeemed. Jesus does not speak of a distant consolation, but of a constant grace that always sustains us, especially in times of affliction. (Pope Leo XIV, Angelus, 1st February 2026)

Word

beatitude /biˈætɪˌtjuːd/ — a state of supreme blessedness or happiness; each of the blessings Jesus pronounces in the Sermon on the Mount / Plain (“Blessed are…”).

From Latin beatitudo (happiness), from beatus “happy, blessed.”

beatitude — Luke’s blessings and woes flip the world’s scoreboard. Leo: happiness is a gift shared, not a prize bought. Seek the poor, the hungry, the weeping — and rejoice when the cost of discipleship shows up.

Skip Hop: Elmo teether recalled for choking risk

9 Sep 2026

Skip Hop’s Sesame Street Elmo silicone teether is under recall in the US and Canada after the black pupil can separate — especially if frozen — and become a choking hazard. ~25k+ units sold across both markets (Jan 2025–Mar 2026). A few incidents, no injuries reported. Stop use; contact Skip Hop for a gift-card refund. Parent check: freeze-friendly teethers still need a parts inventory — anything that can snap off fails the crib test.

Water-bead squeeze toys recalled — expansion risk

9 Sep 2026

CPSC pulled ~8,000 GIHNJSI Squeezy Dumplings and ~7,200 OKK Rainbow Mystery Squishy Bun toys. Both hide water beads that can expand after swallowing — choking and intestinal obstruction, with dehydration and death risk on the warning. Sold on Amazon / third-party shelves May–Aug 2026; no injuries yet. Mark RECALLED, trash, email the maker for a refund. Takeaway: “squishy” + beads is a toddler hazard, not a fidget.

Mathspace breach: 1.08M school accounts exposed

9 Sep 2026

Australian edtech Mathspace says unauthorized parties hit an internal reporting system — 1,079,819 users across Australia and New Zealand (students, staff, parents). Exposed: names, emails, usernames, IDs, country/timezone, last-active. Not exposed (per company): passwords, SSO tokens, grades, school linkage. System offline; schools and individuals being notified. Parent playbook: treat cold “Mathspace” DMs as phishing, unique passwords, watch for account spam.

McD Cebu: five more boxes before 2027

8 Sep 2026

McDonald’s is leaning into Cebu and the rest of Visayas/Mindanao as the next growth engine. The island already has 38 restaurants (first Jones Avenue store 1992). Two opened this year in Marigondon and Tipolo, Mandaue; five more are scheduled before year-end — SRP Talisay, Casamira in Guadalupe, Yati in Liloan, Compostela, plus an ICM Cebu relocation. National network sits around 862 stores; Visayas and Mindanao still punch below their population weight. Energy tell: 17 Cebu restaurants already on Corenergy, with the program eyed for more Visayas/Mindanao sites. Opener/COO read: this is catchment densify in secondary cities, not a Metro vanity push. If you’re south Luzon QSR-adjacent, the same density math applies to your corridors — own a day-part or a protein they don’t, and watch lease inflation near new boxes.

DA: cut the $11B farm trade gap — start with fruit

8 Sep 2026

Agriculture Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel put the country’s ~$11B annual agri trade deficit on the table as DA returned to Asia Fruit Logistica in Hong Kong (2–4 Sep) after nearly a decade away. The show drew 760+ exhibitors and ~14,000 buyers from 80 countries. Pitch: bigger export markets raise farmer incomes and free capital that currently leaks abroad — money that could fund irrigation and productivity. Early focus: dragon fruit logistics and shipping to cut post-harvest loss, with a virtual training track for DA HVC and standards staff. Buyer interest showed up; supply limits blocked some orders — the tell that production scale, not just marketing, is the bottleneck. Entrepreneur read: export lanes that work become templates. If dragon fruit cold chain clears, ask which Batangas/Laguna HVCs can ride the same lane instead of only feeding Metro wet markets.

DA: pork soft is ASF “fastbreak,” not over-import

8 Sep 2026

Tiu Laurel clarified yesterday’s price story: hog raisers sold early ahead of rainy-season ASF risk — a “fastbreak” — so farmgate dropped even as Q2 production rose 5.6% to 412,280 MT. Average farmgate −~19% YoY to P172.44/kg from P211.91. Mindanao prices fell despite little imported pork there. BAI expects 2026 imports down ~50,000 MT vs last year’s 851,760 MT. Commercial ASF vaccine approval is the expansion unlock; a second shot is under review. Kitchen read: soft farmgate is a short buy window for kasim/liempo while disease still sits in most provinces — don’t lock six-month menu cost on today’s quote, and dual-source if Habagat keeps wet-market swings alive.

Tale of the tape

Week three opened with Visma on the front from Cortegana — Kruijswijk and Nordhagen never let the five breathe past three minutes — then Coquard’s Cofidis and Meeus’s Red Bull shared the chase into the Huelva wind. A late split spat Cort out; he clawed back inside 11 km. Into the last kilometre nobody matched van Aert’s launch. Brennan rolled out of the pocket for win four: first Grand Tour debutant with four stages since Gaviria’s Giro 2017, youngest since Vicente Carretero in 1936, Visma’s sixth this Vuelta.

GC slept again. Mas still banks 2:06 on Gall and four more on Roglič into Sevilla’s second sprinter day, then the 32 km Jerez chrono that actually moves the board. Brennan: plan was one win; he has four and is already talking Tour replicas. Van Aert: “Basically, Matthew was always in my pocket… Seven stage wins is something that happens very rarely.”
