Reading of the day

A reading from the Third Letter of John 5-8

Beloved, you are faithful in all you do for the brothers and sisters, especially for strangers; they have testified to your love before the Church. Please help them in a way worthy of God to continue their journey. For they have set out for the sake of the Name and are accepting nothing from the pagans. Gospel of the day

From the Gospel according to Luke 18:1-8

Jesus told his disciples a parable about the necessity for them to pray always without becoming weary. He said, “There was a judge in a certain town who neither feared God nor respected any human being. And a widow in that town used to come to him and say, ‘Render a just decision for me against my adversary.’ For a long time the judge was unwilling, but eventually he thought, ‘While it is true that I neither fear God nor respect any human being, because this widow keeps bothering me I shall deliver a just decision for her lest she finally come and strike me.’” The Lord said, “Pay attention to what the dishonest judge says. Will not God then secure the rights of his chosen ones who call out to him day and night? Will he be slow to answer them? I tell you, he will see to it that justice is done for them speedily. But when the Son of Man comes, will he find faith on earth?”

The words of the Popes

Christ speaks to his disciples of the “need to pray always, and not to lose heart” (Lk 18:1). Just as we never grow weary of breathing, so let us never grow weary of praying! Just as breathing sustains the life of the body, so prayer sustains the life of the soul: faith, in fact, is expressed in prayer, and authentic prayer lives on faith. Jesus shows us this connection with a parable: a judge remains deaf to the pressing requests of a widow, whose perseverance finally leads him to act. At a glance, such tenacity becomes for us a beautiful example of hope, especially in times of trial and tribulation. Nevertheless, the woman’s perseverance and the judge, who acts reluctantly, set the stage for a provocative question from Jesus: Will not God, the good Father, “grant justice to his chosen ones who cry to him day and night?” (Lk 18:7). (…) The Church’s prayer reminds us that God grants justice to all, giving his life for all. Thus, when we cry out to the Lord, “Where are you?”, let us transform this invocation into a prayer, and then we will recognize that God is present where the innocent suffer. The cross of Christ reveals God’s justice, and God’s justice is forgiveness. He sees evil and redeems it by taking it upon himself. When we are “crucified” by pain and violence, by hatred and war, Christ is already there, on the cross for us and with us. There is no cry that God does not console; there is no tear that is far from his heart. The Lord listens to us, embraces us as we are, and transforms us as he is. (Pope Leo XIV, Homily, 19 October 2025)

The Way — Prayer

“Persevere in prayer. Persevere, even when your efforts seem barren. Prayer is always fruitful.”

101 · escriva.org

Word

persevere /ˌpɜːrsəˈvɪər/ (verb) — to continue steadily in a course of action or belief despite difficulty, delay or lack of success.

From Old French perseverer, from Latin perseverare “to continue steadfastly,” from per- “thoroughly” + severus “strict, serious.”

Shopee launches ShopeeFood in the Philippines, expanding its ecosystem of everyday services

13 Nov 2026 · ABS-CBN Lifestyle

Shopee has launched ShopeeFood in Metro Manila, making the Philippines its fifth Southeast Asian market for food delivery. Restaurants on the app range from quick-service chains and local favourites to cafes, bakeries and snack shops, and Shopee says it will bring in more national chains. It is folding food into its usual sale calendar (Weekday Flash Deals, Payday Sales, Double Day Mega Sales). New users get up to 75% off their first order, free delivery and a 50%-off voucher.

Operator read: Shopee already has the customers and the payments, so it will spend to buy orders in the first few months. That makes this a good time to get in early on good terms: low or waived commission, the platform paying for vouchers, and placement in the Payday and Double Day campaigns. Keep the menu on Shopee lean and priced the same as on Grab and foodpanda, so you don't train customers to wait for the cheaper app. It only covers Metro Manila for now. Laguna stores should watch for the rollout, not plan around it.

13 Nov 2026 · 8List.ph

Malaysian beverage brand Forest Cloud has opened its first Philippine store at the ground floor of Greenhills Mall, San Juan.
