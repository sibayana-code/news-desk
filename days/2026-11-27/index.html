Reading of the day

A reading from the Book of Revelation 20:1-4, 11—21:2

I, John, saw an angel come down from heaven, holding in his hand the key to the abyss and a heavy chain. He seized the dragon, the ancient serpent, which is the Devil or Satan, and tied it up for a thousand years and threw it into the abyss, which he locked over it and sealed, so that it could no longer lead the nations astray until the thousand years are completed. After this, it is to be released for a short time. Then I saw thrones; those who sat on them were entrusted with judgment. I also saw the souls of those who had been beheaded for their witness to Jesus and for the word of God, and who had not worshiped the beast or its image nor had accepted its mark on their foreheads or hands. They came to life and they reigned with Christ for a thousand years. Next I saw a large white throne and the one who was sitting on it. The earth and the sky fled from his presence and there was no place for them. I saw the dead, the great and the lowly, standing before the throne, and scrolls were opened. Anyone whose name was not found written in the book of life was thrown into the pool of fire. Then I saw a new heaven and a new earth. The former heaven and the former earth had passed away, and the sea was no more. I also saw the holy city, a new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God, prepared as a bride adorned for her husband.

Gospel of the day

From the Gospel according to Luke 21:29-33

Jesus told his disciples a parable. “Consider the fig tree and all the other trees. When their buds burst open, you see for yourselves and know that summer is now near; in the same way, when you see these things happening, know that the Kingdom of God is near. Amen, I say to you, this generation will not pass away until all these things have taken place. Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not pass away.”

The words of the Popes

In some circumstances in our life, when we are going through a crisis or experience some failure, as well as when we see around us the pain caused by wars, violence, natural disasters, we have the feeling that everything is coming to an end, and we feel that even the most beautiful things pass away. Crises and failures, however, though painful, are important, because they teach us to accord everything its due weight, not to attach our hearts to the realities of this world, because they will pass: they are destined to fade away. At the same time, Jesus talks about what will remain. Everything passes away, but His words will not pass away: Jesus’ words will remain for eternity. He thus invites us to trust in the Gospel, which contains a promise of salvation and eternity, and not to live under the anguish of death. For while everything passes away, Christ remains. In Him, in Christ, we shall one day find again the things and people who have passed away and who have accompanied us in our earthly existence. In the light of this promise of resurrection, every reality takes on a new meaning: everything dies and we too will one day die, but we will lose nothing of what we have built and loved, because death will be the beginning of a new life. (…) Let us not forget that God is preparing for us a future of life and joy. (Pope Francis, Angelus, 17 November 2024)

Word

eternity /ɪˈtɜːrnɪti/ (noun) — infinite or unending time; in theology, the endless life beyond death.

From Old French eternité, from Latin aeternitas, from aeternus “eternal,” from aevum “age, lifetime.”

SM Prime opens the ₱7.7-billion SM Nuvali mall in Laguna

26 Nov 2026 · BusinessWorld

SM Prime opens SM Nuvali in Sta. Rosa on Friday 27 Nov: 229,000 sq.m., designed by Arquitectonica, with a one-hectare indoor garden under an ETFE skylight. It has dining areas opening onto outdoor promenades and an SM Game Park built into the food hall. Elevated walkways link it to Park Inn by Radisson SM Nuvali and SMX Trade Hall SM Nuvali. NV Tower, a Grade A office building going for PEZA accreditation, is under construction next door with retail on its lower floors.

Operator read: a mall with a hotel, events hall and offices attached brings weekday lunch and event catering demand on top of the weekend family crowd. That's the profile to plan for if you're looking at a site or a commissary route near Nuvali. Ayala's Nuvali dining strip is next door, so rents and foot traffic there are worth watching too.

SRA opens voluntary purchase of local raw sugar for crop year 2026–2027

25 Nov 2026 · Philstar

Under Sugar Order 2, farmers, millers, refiners, manufacturers, beverage makers and traders can buy locally produced raw sugar. Buyers get priority in any future sugar import or export allocation, and only sugar bought under the order would qualify for a US export program. The purchased sugar is held as “C” or reserve sugar for up to 90 days. The allocation is split 75% to farmers and 25% to millers. SRA says the aim is to keep farmgate prices reasonable while taming retail prices.

Operator read: big buyers like beverage makers and manufacturers are being rewarded for buying local now with first rights on imports later. That tends to keep sugar prices steady. If you buy sugar in volume for drinks or desserts, ask your supplier whether they're joining the program, because that tells you who will have stock if imports open.
