Food Desk

Rice, storms, inflation

Tape

Rice is the cost problem that does not wait for harvest. About P9/kg more than last August on regular, storms chewed Luzon veg, BSP says August inflation stays hot on food. Pork is the one line that got cheaper. Use that.

September harvest and open imports should stop a spike, not unwind the last twelve months. Treat rice and veg as tight through Q4.

Takes

  • Protect rice-heavy plates. Push pork while bone-in is ~10% cheaper YoY.
  • Dual-source veg away from the eight hit regions. Lock oil and dry stores.
  • Do not raise the whole menu. Put the increase on rice add-ons and oil-heavy fry items.
  • Storm-hit Central Luzon is stressed demand, not a new catchment. Near-term traffic is still Metro malls.

La Vuelta · Stage 7

Uno-X 1-2 on a day of Norwegian mourning

Tale of the tape

Van Aert and Leknessund went from kilometre zero. On Valdelinares Leknessund punched with 7.5 km left; Johannessen sat on the chase then dropped van Aert for the 1-2. Norway's first Vuelta stage since Hushovd in 2010, Uno-X's first Vuelta win on debut.

GC sat seven minutes back until Gall lit the last two km. Pogačar answered, dropped Mas and Roglič, and padded the lead to 3:50 / 4:50. Green flipped on van Aert's all-day raid.

Takes

  • Leknessund: "I didn't expect it to be this emotional… Tobias let my wheel go and basically gave me the win." Quotes
  • van Aert "paid it on the last climb," still: "he really deserves this victory."
  • Pogačar: "We're here to win the general classification and not fool around."
  • Tomorrow: Stage 8 Puçol → Xeraco, ~171 km, one late cat. 2.
