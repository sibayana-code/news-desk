Reading of the day

A reading from the Letter of St. Paul to the Galatians 3:22-29

Brothers and sisters: Scripture confined all things under the power of sin, that through faith in Jesus Christ the promise might be given to those who believe. Before faith came, we were held in custody under law, confined for the faith that was to be revealed. Consequently, the law was our disciplinarian for Christ, that we might be justified by faith. But now that faith has come, we are no longer under a disciplinarian. For through faith you are all children of God in Christ Jesus. For all of you who were baptized into Christ have clothed yourselves with Christ. There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither slave nor free person, there is not male and female; for you are all one in Christ Jesus. And if you belong to Christ, then you are Abraham’s descendants, heirs according to the promise.

Gospel of the day

From the Gospel according to Luke 11:27-28

While Jesus was speaking, a woman from the crowd called out and said to him, “Blessed is the womb that carried you and the breasts at which you nursed.” He replied, “Rather, blessed are those who hear the word of God and observe it.”

The words of the Popes

To listen to the Word of God and practise it, are the only two “conditions” established by Jesus for those who want to follow him. Christian life is “simple, simple”. Perhaps we have made it a bit difficult, with so many explanations that nobody understands, but the Christian life is like this: listening to the Word of God and practising it. (…) Every time we open the Gospel and read a passage and ask ourselves: ‘Is God speaking to me with this, is he saying something to me’?. This means “to listen to the Word of God, to listen with the ears and listen with the heart, to open the heart to God’s Word”. On the other hand, “Jesus’ enemies listened to Jesus’ words but they were close by in order to try and find a mistake, to make him slip up” and make him lose authority. But they never asked themselves: ‘What is God telling me with these words’?. Moreover, the Pontiff added, “God does not speak only to all but he speaks to each one of us. The Gospel was written for each one of us. (…) The Lord, “always plants his Word”, and in exchange “he asks only for an open heart to listen to it and good will to put it into practise. This is why today’s prayer is that of the Psalm: ‘Guide me, Lord, in the way of your commands’, that is, on the path of your Word, so that, with your guidance, I may learn to practice it”. (Pope Francis, Santa Marta, 23 September 2014)

Hog raisers want pork duties back to 30–40% now; DA’s chief agrees but can’t sign alone

9 Oct 2026

PNA and Manila Times: At a Friday briefing, the Samahang Industriya ng Agrikultura (SINAG), the National Federation of Hog Farmers and United Batangas Swine Raisers, among others, asked President Marcos to restore pork tariffs immediately to 30% for imports within the minimum access volume and 40% outside it. Rates now sit at 15% and 25% under Executive Order 62, which otherwise runs to 2028. The groups reject the Department of Agriculture’s filed proposal, which steps up to 25% and 35% in January 2027 and reaches 30% and 40% in 2028. They also want tighter quarantine and inspection, and weekly updates of import reference values. On Thursday, Agriculture Secretary Francisco Tiu Laurel Jr. said that, going by the data, he wants the higher rates now, but the decision is shared with the rest of the national government. He said a cabinet decision could come Friday; none had been reported by this morning. Bureau of Animal Industry figures show 1.14 billion kg of meat imported in January to August, more than half of it pork at 602.65 million kg, up 5.18% from a year earlier. DA says local hog production grew 6% in the first half even as farmgate prices fell. Separately, DA has a Tariff Commission petition that could open a window for a temporary import quota, and a bid to reclassify pork jowl, the sisig cut, at a higher duty. Operator read: this is the one cost line that is soft right now, and every path points up. A kitchen buying imported belly, jowl or trimmings is getting a price that exists because of a temporary tariff. If the immediate restore goes through, landed cost on imported pork can rise by up to 15 points of duty in one move; even the slow path adds 10 points on New Year’s Day. Lock 2027 volume with your meat supplier before importers reprice on the news, and test a local-pork spec now while live-hog prices are weak. For a concept with sisig as the hero, the jowl reclassification is the specific risk to price into the menu.

Figaro, owner of Angel’s Pizza, leaves the PSE with a ₱0.82 bank-funded buyout

9 Oct 2026

Bilyonaryo: Jerry Liu’s Figaro Culinary Group (FCG) shares jumped 16.4% to ₱0.78 on Friday after its subsidiary Figaro Coffee Systems Inc. (FCSI) offered ₱0.82 a share to minority holders ahead of a voluntary delisting. That is a 22.4% premium to Thursday’s close and above the ₱0.75 IPO price of January 2022, when the company raised ₱767 million. The board, including three independent directors, approved the delisting on Oct 7, and shareholders vote at a special meeting on Nov 13. FCSI is funding the tender with a senior secured term loan from China Banking Corp, whose investment-banking arm underwrote the IPO. Monde Nissin, which paid ₱820.3 million for 15% in early 2023 to get into food service through Angel’s Pizza, keeps its stake and is excluded from the offer along with holding companies Carmetheus and Camerton. The offerors need 95% of outstanding shares to finish. In October 2024 Figaro had floated a follow-on offering to fund expansion; it has not said why it dropped that. Operator read: going private on debt is a bet that the brands are worth more than the stock says and that management can move faster without quarterly scrutiny. The Angel’s Pizza spin-off this desk flagged on 31 Aug now reads as part of the same restructuring. For a mid-size Filipino chain, the lesson is that the small-cap PSE route has not paid much: the exit price is only 9% above an IPO price set almost five years ago. Private credit and strategic partners like Monde are the funding path that worked here. For a franchise buyer, wait for the post-delisting plan before signing anything with Angel’s or Figaro, because store and brand priorities may change.
